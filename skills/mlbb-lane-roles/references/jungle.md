# Jungle Role Macro
Sources: JUNG
Scope: Jungler macro by archetype (tank jungler vs damage jungler) across draft, early, mid and late game: spell choice, routes and invades, objective (turtle/Lord) contests via the retribution fight, teamfight job, ahead/behind behaviour, mistakes, coordination. Does NOT cover: specific hero kits, item builds, spawn timers, retribution numbers (all in the LIVE table), other roles' macro (see `exp-lane.md`, `gold-lane.md`, `mid-lane.md`, `roam.md`).

## 1. Role purpose by archetype
- Everything in jungle macro depends on the jungler type: tank jungler or damage jungler (assassin or fighter). Routes, farming and teamfight job all differ. (src: JUNG 00:25)
- Tank jungler: slow clear; gets ahead by DISRUPTING the enemy's farm (steals enemy monsters, forces small fights); front line in fights; becomes the secondary tank late. (src: JUNG 05:09, 05:27, 05:38, 06:30, 07:31)
- Damage jungler: fast clear; gets ahead by OUT-FARMING the opponent (own jungle plus empty-lane minions, alone); waits and enters teamfights last. (src: JUNG 04:27, 05:38, 07:00)
- Purpose of both: win the retribution fights for objectives (turtle early, Lord late). Farm level advantage exists to win those fights. (src: JUNG 04:52, 08:15)

## 2. Draft and spell choice
- IF the team does not yet have two tank heroes THEN pick a tank jungler; IF the team already has enough tanks THEN pick a damage jungler (assassin/fighter). (src: JUNG 00:25)
- After that, stop focusing on jungler type and focus on team composition: (src: JUNG 00:50)
  - Dive comp -> a jungler who can jump onto enemies.
  - Front-to-back comp -> a DPS jungler.
  - Unsure of the comp -> a flexible jungler that can do both.
  - The video's hero examples for each comp are dropped; the classification must be re-checked at use time → L-JUNG-01.
- BECAUSE early farming routes depend on the hero you play. (src: JUNG 01:22)
- The fight starts at the first buy: choose the retribution variant by the hero's needs (L-JUNG-02): (src: JUNG 01:37)
  - Hero needs extra mobility or needs to kite -> the mobility/kiting variant.
  - Hero is agile and wants to 1v1 all the time -> the 1v1 variant.
  - Playing a tank jungler -> the tank variant.

## 3. Early game
### 3.1 Route and pathing
1. In the early game always START from the GOLD lane side and finish toward the XP lane side. Two start options exist (blue-side or red-side buff); which one you use follows where the gold lane is. (src: JUNG 01:54, 02:05)
2. Before minute 5 the farming route is the same for all junglers (timing: L-JUNG-06). (src: JUNG 04:17)
3. After all monsters are cleared, rotate to the XP lane and stay next to the XP laner. The XP laner is the one who carries your early game. (src: JUNG 02:40)
- ⚠ unverified: exact camp order of the route was not readable in the video (animated minimap only). (src: JUNG 02:00)

### 3.2 Invades
- IF the enemy invades your jungle THEN look at the matchup: win the matchup -> fight them; lose the matchup -> do not die, run away and farm somewhere else. The same logic applies when YOU invade. (src: JUNG 02:17)

### 3.3 Early objective: the turtle
- The key is the LAST-HIT DAMAGE matchup against the enemy jungler (Turtle numbers: L-JUNG-10). Last-hit damage = retribution damage + your hardest-hitting finishing skill (values: L-JUNG-03). (src: JUNG 02:54, 03:08)
- IF you win the last-hit matchup THEN focus the turtle (while your roamer and XP laner zone the enemy). IF you lose it THEN focus on fighting the enemy instead and teamfight first, taking the objective later. BECAUSE the enemy with higher last-hit damage wins the retribution fight easily if they come in. (src: JUNG 03:18, 03:47)
  - Worked example (Round 1/2): a damage jungler whose finishing skill plus Retribution is higher than a tank jungler's can focus the objective while roamer and XP laner zone; the tank jungler cannot contest it and should teamfight first, taking the objective later. (numbers → L-JUNG-04)
- IF you can neither out-last-hit nor out-fight the enemy jungler (Round 2/2 example: an early-weak damage jungler against a tank jungler; numbers → L-JUNG-04) THEN "crush the map": go into the enemy jungle far from the turtle, steal their monsters, and you can even gank the enemy gold laner. (src: JUNG 03:54)
- IF your XP laner snowballs THEN you will easily get the turtle, because they can zone and pick off enemies. (src: JUNG 02:47)

## 4. Mid game (after the early phase)
- Damage jungler: (src: JUNG 04:27)
  1. Clear speed is fast: clear the jungle monsters first, then farm minions ALONE in an empty lane. (src: JUNG 04:27, 04:47)
  2. Usually take the XP lane's minions while the XP laner rotates to another lane. Avoid the gold lane because they need to farm too. (src: JUNG 04:37)
  3. Farm alone so your XP is not shared. (src: JUNG 04:47)
  4. Why: higher level = more retribution damage = easier retribution fight (L-JUNG-05). (src: JUNG 04:52)
- Tank jungler: (src: JUNG 05:09)
  1. Clears slowly. Do NOT farm lane minions. (Reason: the tank jungler's damage comes from an item passive that only triggers on jungle monsters, L-JUNG-07. ⚠ conflict: the slide carrying this text was titled for damage junglers; the audio says tank junglers, and the audio is followed.) (src: JUNG 05:09, 05:24)
  2. Shines in the mid game: enter the enemy jungle to steal their monsters, pressure the enemy, force small fights. (src: JUNG 05:27)

## 5. Retribution-fight method (objectives)
- A retribution level advantage still has to be executed well. Steps: (src: JUNG 05:43)
  1. Calculate your last-hit damage (retribution + hardest-hitting skill). (src: JUNG 05:52)
  2. Position properly: do not stand inside the pit or anywhere the enemy can easily target you; stand at the outer edge. (src: JUNG 06:00)
  3. CC the enemy jungler as much as possible right before retribution so the enemy loses their window. (src: JUNG 06:07)
  4. IF you have no CC THEN ask your team for help. (src: JUNG 06:16)

## 6. Teamfights (mid game onward)
- The team must be coordinated, especially in teamfights; your job depends on your hero type. (src: JUNG 06:21)
- Tank jungler: (src: JUNG 06:30)
  - Stand at the front line. (src: JUNG 06:30)
  - Main job: protect your marksman and mage, while preparing CC to punish the enemy marksman if they step out of position. (src: JUNG 06:50)
  - IF you show yourself on the map THEN make sure the team is ready to back you up first. BECAUSE if you get picked off, you sit on a respawn timer and the enemy takes the objective. (src: JUNG 06:34, 06:45)
- Damage jungler (enter LAST): (src: JUNG 07:00)
  - Never show yourself on the map. Hide alone in the bush, because a single CC can get you picked off. (src: JUNG 07:00)
  - Your job is to wait: wait for the enemy to engage and flank them, or wait for your tank to start and follow up. (src: JUNG 07:07)
  - Target priority: marksman, then jungler, then mage. (src: JUNG 07:23)

## 7. Late game
- Tank jungler: no longer the main tank but the SECONDARY tank ("a walking retribution"). Show yourself with the team and be ready to fight. (src: JUNG 07:31)
- Tank jungler must NOT clear a lane far from the Lord. IF you are seen far from the Lord THEN the enemy takes it immediately. (src: JUNG 07:44)
- Damage jungler: may still clear a far lane IF you have high mobility. (src: JUNG 07:54)
  - But do not show yourself on the map, because whoever wins CC wins and your team will lose. (src: JUNG 08:03)
  - In the fight, position as far as possible from the enemy initiator and get ready. (src: JUNG 08:10)
- The key to winning the late game is the Lord; know the "lord dance". (src: JUNG 08:15)

### 7.1 Lord dance (retribution advantage in an even game)
- IF the situation is even THEN the Lord cannot be burst because retribution is a coin-flip (probability illustration: L-JUNG-08). The lord dance, executed properly, gives you a retribution advantage. (src: JUNG 08:22, 08:29)
1. Slow-push the far lane first. (src: JUNG 08:37)
2. Set up vision. (src: JUNG 08:41)
3. Open the map near the jungle and hit the Lord slowly; do NOT stand inside the Lord pit. (src: JUNG 08:42)
4. Keep hitting the Lord while opening the map in the middle of the river until some enemies are revealed. (src: JUNG 08:48)
- Goal: NOT to finish the Lord, but to make the enemy confused between the Lord and the pushed far lane. (src: JUNG 08:53)
- Poke the enemy lightly while stalling the Lord. (src: JUNG 09:02)
- The pushed far lane forces the enemy to send someone to catch the wave; that opens a brief window for your team to start a fight. (src: JUNG 09:05)
- IF you cannot start the fight THEN keep poking lightly and repeat until someone blunders. (src: JUNG 09:15)

## 8. Ahead vs behind (summary of stated behaviour)
- Ahead (higher last-hit / farm lead): focus the objective while allies zone; damage junglers convert the farm lead into retribution fights. (src: JUNG 03:47, 04:52)
- Behind in last-hit: fight first, objective later. (src: JUNG 03:47)
- Behind in matchup when invaded: do not die; farm elsewhere. (src: JUNG 02:17)
- Behind in both last-hit and fighting: crush the map (steal far enemy monsters, possible gold-lane gank). (src: JUNG 03:54)
- Even late game: lord dance instead of a retribution coin-flip. (src: JUNG 08:22)

## 9. Mistakes and fixes
- MISTAKE 1: clearing lane minions in the early game (most fatal early mistake) — the jungler's gold is cut (L-JUNG-09). FIX: clear jungle monsters only, rotate to the XP lane after. (src: JUNG 02:32, 02:40)
- MISTAKE 2 (tank jungler, super common): showing yourself on the map and getting picked off; while you respawn the enemy takes the objective. FIX: show only when the team can back you up. (src: JUNG 06:34, 06:45)
- MISTAKE 3 (tank jungler, late game): clearing a lane far from the Lord; the enemy takes the Lord the moment you are seen far away. FIX: stay near the Lord; join the team as secondary tank. (src: JUNG 07:44)
- MISTAKE (implied): standing inside the pit during a retribution fight, where enemies can easily target you. FIX: stand outside the pit, CC the enemy jungler right before retribution. (src: JUNG 06:00)
- MISTAKE (implied): damage jungler showing on the map before the fight. FIX: hide alone in the bush and enter last. (src: JUNG 07:00)

## 10. Coordination with other roles
- XP laner: join after the first clear; a snowballing XP laner gives you the turtle by zoning. (src: JUNG 02:40, 02:47)
- Roamer and XP laner zone the enemy while the jungler (with last-hit advantage) takes the turtle. (src: JUNG 03:18)
- Team comp (dive / front-to-back) steers jungler choice. (src: JUNG 00:50)
- Tank jungler protects marksman and mage in fights; punishes the enemy marksman with prepared CC. (src: JUNG 06:50)
- Damage jungler follows up the tank's engagement or flanks after the enemy engages. (src: JUNG 07:07)
- Ask the team for CC during a retribution fight if you have none. (src: JUNG 06:16)
- Late game: the team's far lane pushing and vision set up the lord dance. (src: JUNG 08:37)
- Gank/vision/peel: the video gives no standalone gank rules besides the gold-lane gank under "crush the map" and the vision step of the lord dance; no other claims recorded.
