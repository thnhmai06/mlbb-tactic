# Role: Gold lane
Sources: GOLD (primary), with cross-references to MACRO, TERM, EMB, DRAFT
Scope: Draft by team composition and rank, the emblem decision after the pick, power-spike-driven decisions, early-game farming and fight-or-flight, surviving a multi-man gank, objective (Turtle) participation rules, mid-game split push/farm and late-game duties. Hero names, emblem/talent names and timers are removed (abstraction rule); what must be re-checked is in the LIVE table. This is a beginner guide in the video: "more play brings understanding of each hero's details" (GOLD 03:34).

## 1. Draft
### 1a. In a full 5-man party: pick by team composition (categories → L-GOLD-01)
- Teammates may not know the theory, so the guide's table is simply followed. (src: GOLD 00:55)
- IF your team has a lot of ranged heroes (poke comp) THEN pick a long-range gold laner. (src: GOLD 01:08)
- IF your team has two or three tanky heroes (front-to-back comp) THEN pick a high-DPS gold laner. (src: GOLD 01:13)
- IF your team has dive-type heroes (dive comp) THEN pick a mobile gold laner. (src: GOLD 01:25)

### 1b. Not in a 5-man party: depends on rank
- IF you are in the top rank tier the video names (L-GOLD-02) or above THEN fill your teammates' weaknesses using the composition theory above; your gold laner does not have to be a marksman or mage. (src: GOLD 01:39, 02:15)
  - MISTAKE (#1/4): picking heroes that rely heavily on communication. (src: GOLD 01:46)
  - Worked example, "High Rank Draft": the other four slots are already filled and the gold-lane slot is empty (the video named the four heroes; dropped); picking a marksman would make the team "too squishy", so pick a fighter-type gold laner instead. (src: GOLD 02:00–02:15)
- IF below that rank THEN pick a hero that can carry. (src: GOLD 02:27)
- Damage-type balance: IF the team already has a lot of magic heroes THEN pick physical, and vice versa. Worked example "Low Rank Draft": your team is mostly magic and your slot is physical-capable → you pick physical. (src: GOLD 02:30–02:36; same rule in EXP 01:39)
- Mindset: "think of the game as 1 vs 9" — your hero must be able to carry on its own. (src: GOLD 02:36)

### 1c. Emblem decision right after the pick
- Compare your gold laner with the enemy gold laner immediately: (src: GOLD 02:43)
  - IF you are weaker in the early game THEN use a defensive emblem setup. (src: GOLD 02:53; the specific choice → L-GOLD-03)
  - IF you are even or stronger early THEN use the role emblem that fits the hero (marksman, assassin or mage). (src: GOLD 03:02)
- Cross-reference: emblem logic in skill `mlbb-emblems` → `emblems.md`.

## 2. Power spike (the basis of everything below)
- Every decision below depends on the hero's power spike: gold laners are strong in early, mid or late game. The video's chart groups gold-laner icons into early (red), mid (blue) and late (green) boxes. (src: GOLD 03:15–03:40; contents → L-GOLD-04; ⚠ unverified: icons unidentified)
- WHY it matters: the power spike decides your early-lane fight/flight, your Turtle decisions and your mid-game lane choice. (src: GOLD 06:55)

## 3. Early game (up to about minute 5; L-GOLD-05)
- Core job: farm while harassing your lane opponent. (src: GOLD 03:49)
- Lane zones: safe, neutral, dangerous (green / yellow / red on the video's overlay; safe = near your turret/base, dangerous = enemy side). (src: GOLD 03:58)
- Always track the enemy mid, roamer and jungler on the minimap: (src: GOLD 04:02)
  - IF one disappears THEN move to the neutral zone. (04:08)
  - IF two disappear THEN fall back to the safe zone. (04:12)
- MISTAKE (#2/4): joining the early skirmishes (the video's phrase is garbled). FIX: that is the mid, jungler and roamer's job; farm and harass your own lane. (src: GOLD 04:15; ⚠ unverified: exact term garbled)

### Fight or flight in the 1v1 (TIP 3/5)
The most important thing is to know when to fight and when to flee. Three cases: (src: GOLD 04:30)
- Stronger early: (src: GOLD 04:38)
  - freeze the minions and zone the opponent so they cannot reach the area where they gain gold and exp from dying minions; they starve of gold and exp;
  - do not overextend; keep checking the enemy mid, jungler and roamer.
- Weaker early: play safe, farm as much as you can in the safe zone; "it's better to miss a last hit than die". (src: GOLD 05:05–05:15)
- Even: (src: GOLD 05:20)
  - farm and poke as much as possible;
  - IF the enemy mid, jungler and roamer are all visible AND your opponent is low THEN go for the kill;
  - IF the lane stays even THEN wait for your team to gank; the key to a successful gank is to freeze the wave near your turret (05:36).
  - Cue for a losing lane position: the enemy hero and minions press up to your turret while you stand by your own minions (05:28).

## 4. Surviving a multi-man gank / defending
- IF you are ganked by four, your hero does not matter; watch the minimap. IF the enemy mid, jungler and roamer disappear for too long THEN fall back to your inner turret. (src: GOLD 05:46, 05:52)
- BIGGEST MISTAKE (#3/4): trying to defend the turret. FIX: let them take the plating or even the turret as long as you stay alive. (src: GOLD 06:01)

## 5. Objective (Turtle) participation rules
- First Turtle (early): same logic as the turret — you do not need to join; stay safe in lane. Join only if you can participate without missing minions (TIP 4/5), e.g. a teammate's skill brings you there. (src: GOLD 06:11–06:27; the exact condition in the video is garbled, ⚠ unverified; examples → L-GOLD-06)
- Second Turtle (early): it can spawn in the gold lane or the XP lane. (src: GOLD 06:27)
  - IF it spawns in the gold lane THEN you must help: do not die before it spawns, clear your minions, move toward it safely, and deal as much damage as possible to it or the enemies while staying alive. (src: GOLD 06:37–06:48)
  - IF it spawns in the XP lane THEN use your power spike (src: GOLD 06:55):
    - strong early → you can help, but only after pushing your wave; IF the enemy zones you so you cannot push THEN skip it and farm;
    - strong late → do not help; push your lane instead.
- FATAL MISTAKE (#4/4): overextending and getting solo-killed; if the team then loses the objective they tilt and the game goes very badly. Priority is to stay alive. (src: GOLD 07:15)
- Third Turtle (mid game): you must help. Rules (TIP 5/5): (src: GOLD 08:17)
  1. do not hit the Turtle without your jungler (GOLD 08:21);
  2. do not show on the map before it spawns (08:27);
  3. do not stay in a far lane before the fight (08:29);
  4. do not take the buff when your team is already ready for war (spoken only, 08:31; buff → L-GOLD-07).

## 6. Mid game
- Goal unchanged: get as much gold as possible, but now you rotate. (src: GOLD 07:33; ⚠ unverified: meaning of a "+100 gold" slide marker)
- IF your hero has mobility THEN split push an outer turret that still stands (L-GOLD-08); IF not THEN farm a lane. (src: GOLD 07:41–07:57; examples → dropped)
- Which lane to farm: the best is mid, because minions reach the centre faster; IF your mid laner does not let you THEN farm whichever lane still has its outer turret. (src: GOLD 08:01–08:13)
- Cross-reference: split push as a team strategy in skill `mlbb-tactics` → `macro-core.md` §9.

## 7. Late game
- Simple macro: clear waves on the pushing lanes and catch enemy waves. (src: GOLD 08:45)
- NEVER walk into the fog of war alone; you will be ambushed; stay close to your team. (src: GOLD 08:57)
- Teamfight job (src: GOLD 09:01–09:24):
  1. survive until the teamfight, that is when you shine;
  2. do not dive or pick off; deal as much consistent damage as possible from a safe position, even if you are hitting tanks or fighters;
  3. IF you see a chance to target enemy damage dealers THEN capitalise on it.
