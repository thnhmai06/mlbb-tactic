# Roam Role Macro
Sources: ROAM
Scope: Roamer macro by archetype (tank, heal, damage roamer) across draft, roam-item choice, early game (before/after first turtle, second turtle), mid game, teamfight task and late game (lord). Does NOT cover: specific hero kits, item stats, exact timers (LIVE table), other roles' macro (see `exp-lane.md`, `gold-lane.md`, `mid-lane.md`, `jungle.md`).

## 1. Role purpose by archetype
- Three roamer types; every decision (rotation path, job at turtle, teamfight task, late-game job) depends on which type you play. Learn the type before the macro. (src: ROAM 00:30, 01:25)
  - Tank roamer: absorbs a lot of damage; goes full tank with no damage items. (src: ROAM 00:50)
  - Heal roamer: heals or shields allies. (src: ROAM 01:05)
  - Damage roamer: deals a ton of damage; burst-skill based. (src: ROAM 01:28)
- The video's example heroes per type are dropped; the categories to re-check → L-ROAM-02.

## 2. Draft and roam-item choice
- IF the team has fewer than 2 tank heroes THEN pick a tank roamer. IF the team already has 2 tanks THEN pick heal or damage roamer. (src: ROAM 00:30, 00:50)
- Heal vs damage: look at the meta and prioritise whichever type the meta favours. (src: ROAM 01:05; meta claim → L-ROAM-01)
- Roam item follows type and strategy (item names dropped; categories → L-ROAM-03): (src: ROAM 01:40)
  - Heal roamer: always the heal-type roam item. (src: ROAM 01:40)
  - Tank or damage roamer: IF dive / pick-off comp THEN the fast-rotation item; IF poke or front-to-back comp THEN the poke/F2B item. (src: ROAM 01:45, 01:55, 01:58)

## 3. Early game before the first turtle (pathing by type)
### 3.1 Tank roamer
1. Start: help the mid laner clear the first wave. (src: ROAM 02:05)
2. Then split by hero early strength (L-ROAM-02): (src: ROAM 02:10)
   - "Mobile and strong" hero: invade the enemy jungler first, then rotate to the gold lane and deal damage to the gold laner for a bit, then rotate to mid and exp lane.
   - "Weak" early hero: rotate straight to the exp lane (no invade, no gold-lane stop).
3. Regardless of hero, focus on controlling the river: open all bushes, light up the map; IF an enemy passes through THEN attack and block their rotation. (src: ROAM 02:35)
- BECAUSE a strong early hero profits from an invade, and river control blocks enemy rotations. (src: ROAM 02:05)

### 3.2 Heal roamer
- Do not open the map or push forward too much: your early game is fragile. (src: ROAM 02:45)
1. Start: help clear the first mid wave. (src: ROAM 02:55)
2. Then decide by whether the hero has damage (L-ROAM-02): (src: ROAM 02:55, 03:10)
   - Hero with "decent damage" early: rotate to the exp lane so it is easier to win the turtle.
   - Hero with "no damage": rotate to the gold lane. BECAUSE you would be useless in the exp lane and might even feed the enemy exp laner.
- Exception stated for one hero (L-ROAM-02): skip helping mid, go straight to the gold lane and stick to the enemy gold laner to starve them of gold ("make the enemy gold laner broke"); keep doing it until the turtle spawns. (src: ROAM 03:22)
  - ⚠ unverified: the hero name for this exception is a whisper spelling.

### 3.3 Damage roamer
- Always open the map toward the enemy buff in the early game (different from heal roamer). (src: ROAM 03:39)
- IF the enemy roamer does NOT help the enemy jungler THEN 1v1 the enemy jungler to try to steal their buff. (src: ROAM 03:39)
- IF the enemy roamer DOES help the jungler THEN just open the map as much as possible, then rotate to the gold lane. (src: ROAM 03:45)
- On arrival at the gold lane you can finish off the enemy gold laner right away, because of your damage. (src: ROAM 03:57)
- TIP: never stay in one lane. Each time you rotate to a lane, unload your burst skills (L-ROAM-02), then escape and move to another lane to do the same. (src: ROAM 04:05)

## 4. First turtle (objective job by type)
- Your focus changes when the first turtle spawns. (src: ROAM 04:25)
- Tank roamer: open all bushes around the turtle. IF the enemy jungler is there THEN go all in, use all skills to zone them and do not let them through. (src: ROAM 04:33)
- Heal roamer: the focus is NOT zoning the enemy jungler; keep YOUR jungler alive as long as possible and focus all heals on them. (src: ROAM 04:45)
- Damage roamer: focus on finishing off the enemy jungler. Do not open the map like a tank roamer; be ready to go all in when the enemy jungler shows. (src: ROAM 04:57)

## 5. Rotation after the first turtle
- Follow the earlier rotation logic, with this focus: (src: ROAM 05:14)
  | Roamer | Focus after the turtle |
  |---|---|
  | Tank | Control the map and help the lane closest to the turtle |
  | Heal | Help the mid and gold laners |
  | Damage | Burst the enemy side laner (⚠ unverified: on-screen row partly unreadable; transcript wording used) |

## 6. Second turtle
- It spawns a fixed time after the first (L-ROAM-04); pay close attention. (src: ROAM 05:30)
- Tank or damage roamer: focus on finishing off the enemy jungler. (src: ROAM 05:38)
  - Do not be stingy with spells; use everything to finish the enemy jungler. (src: ROAM 05:46)
- Heal roamer: the focus is no longer the jungler but YOUR EXP laner; use all heal and utility on them so they can zone safely. (src: ROAM 05:59)
- Repeat the same rotation and turtle strategy until the early game ends. (src: ROAM 06:08)

## 7. Mid game
- Team rotation has changed, so adjust yours. (src: ROAM 06:17)
- Tank roamer: you are super tanky because the enemy does not have penetration yet (L-ROAM-05), so do not be afraid to "make a mess" and open the map. (src: ROAM 06:24)
- Heal roamer: stick with your mid laner. BECAUSE you two are easy to pick off, together you protect each other; never go alone since the enemy jungler is at its strongest in the mid game. (src: ROAM 06:32)
  - Only open the map if you have the right skills (L-ROAM-02). (src: ROAM 06:45)
- Damage roamer: the mid game is when you shine. BECAUSE the enemy mage and marksman lack defense items while you already have two items, so do not hesitate to fight. (src: ROAM 06:59; L-ROAM-05)

## 8. Teamfight task
| Roamer | Task | src |
|---|---|---|
| Tank | Look for mistakes by enemy damage dealers (standing in front of the tank, walking into bushes, not keeping distance); go all in when they are out of position | ROAM 07:15 |
| Heal | Protect your damage dealer: always stand near them | ROAM 07:15 |
| Damage | Flank the enemy damage dealer and pick them off, raising the chance of winning | ROAM 07:15 |
- Learn this because it carries into the late game where rotation is built around the Lord. (src: ROAM 07:48)

## 9. Late game (rotation around the Lord)
- Tank roamer: (src: ROAM 07:55)
  - Always stay in the jungle area close to the Lord.
  - Look at your marksman: IF the marksman is flanking THEN follow and flank with them; IF not THEN focus on opening the map around the Lord.
  - Do not worry about the far lane. Do not open a fight alone; make sure the team is ready to follow up first.
  - Target the enemy marksman. Do not be stingy with skills and do not wait for the perfect moment; go all in on the marksman.
- Heal roamer: (src: ROAM 08:36)
  - Stay in the back; let tank heroes open the map and mobile heroes clear the far lane.
  - Keep your marksman alive as long as possible; stick to them wherever they are.
- Damage roamer: (src: ROAM 08:51)
  - You may clear the far lane. IF your hero can push lane well (L-ROAM-02) THEN split-push to bait the enemy back before rotating straight to the Lord.
  - Careful: the enemy now has defense items, so you cannot burst them like in the early/mid game.

## 10. Ahead vs behind
- The video states no explicit ahead/behind rules for the roamer. Condition-based branches it does state: weak early hero -> go straight to exp lane; no-damage heal roamer -> gold lane; strong tank hero -> invade; mid game tankiness advantage because the enemy lacks penetration; late game enemy defense items remove the damage roamer's burst advantage. (src: ROAM 02:10, 03:10, 06:24, 08:51)

## 11. Mistakes and fixes
- MISTAKE: hesitating at the second turtle while waiting for the "right moment" to commit on the enemy jungler. This gets YOUR jungler picked off first. FIX: commit with all spells, do not be stingy. (src: ROAM 05:46)
- MISTAKE: heal roamer opening the map or pushing early (fragile early game). FIX: do not open the map; help mid then pick exp/gold lane by damage. (src: ROAM 02:45)
- MISTAKE: heal roamer with no damage going to the exp lane, where you are useless and may feed the enemy exp laner. FIX: gold lane. (src: ROAM 03:10)
- MISTAKE: heal roamer going alone in the mid game. FIX: stick with the mid laner. (src: ROAM 06:32)
- MISTAKE: damage roamer staying in one lane. FIX: burst, escape, rotate to another lane. (src: ROAM 04:05)
- MISTAKE: damage roamer opening the map like a tank at the turtle. FIX: stay ready to go all in. (src: ROAM 04:57)
- MISTAKE: tank roamer opening a fight alone in the late game. FIX: only start when the team is ready to follow up. (src: ROAM 07:55)
- MISTAKE: damage roamer trying to burst in the late game like earlier. FIX: remember the enemy has defense items. (src: ROAM 08:51)

## 12. Coordination with other roles
- Mid laner: all types help clear the first mid wave; heal roamer sticks with the mid laner in the mid game. (src: ROAM 02:05, 02:55, 06:32)
- Jungler: heal roamer keeps their own jungler alive at the first turtle; tank/damage roamer zone or finish the enemy jungler. (src: ROAM 04:33, 04:45, 04:57)
- Exp laner: heal roamer with damage joins exp lane early; heal roamer focuses the exp laner at the second turtle. (src: ROAM 02:55, 05:59)
- Gold laner: no-damage heal roamer goes to gold lane; rotation after turtle helps mid and gold laners; damage roamer bursts the enemy gold laner. (src: ROAM 03:10, 05:14, 03:57)
- Marksman: heal roamer sticks to the marksman late; tank roamer follows if the marksman flanks; both target the enemy marksman. (src: ROAM 08:36, 07:55)
- Damage dealer: heal roamer protects them in fights. (src: ROAM 07:15)
- Team readiness: tank roamer starts a war only when the team can follow up. (src: ROAM 07:55)
- Vision: tank roamer opens bushes/river early and around the Lord; heal roamer avoids it early; opening the map needs the right skills in the mid game. (src: ROAM 02:35, 02:45, 06:45, 07:55)
