# MLBB micro mechanics notebook

Levels of hero mastery, from easy (gold gap) to hard (hidden mechanics). Mechanisms are kept; the video's hero examples are not. Full details: `references/micro-mechanics.md`.

## Quick card
- Damage type comes from the skill, not the item; buy by the colour of the skill's scaling text. (src: HID 00:30–01:40)
- Easy level: win the gold gap; last hit and deny; poke the enemy when they step up to last hit. (src: HID 01:10–01:25)
- Medium: spacing (longer range pokes) and cooldown-dependent range: wait for the enemy's long-range skill to be spent. (src: HID 02:10–02:40)
- Medium: power spikes by level and items; find the enemy's main damage source and play around it. (src: HID 02:55–03:55)
- Hard: animation cancel, basic-attack reset, hook redirect with a blink, and queuing a conditional ability before the blink (input buffer). (src: HID 04:10–06:25)
- Whether a given hero still has a mechanic must be checked in practice mode.

## How to coach with this notebook
- Find the rule in the reference, then answer with the decision, the *why*, and the mistake to avoid. Keep it short, reply in the player's language, and quote the source tag `(src: VIDEO mm:ss)` when it helps.
- The notebook names no heroes or items on purpose: it describes them by role, archetype or property. When the player names a hero or item, map it to the property yourself and say that hero/item details should be checked on mlbbhub.com or the MLBB wiki (mobile-legends.fandom.com).
- Numbers, timers and "this category exists" claims sit in the **LIVE-DATA TO RE-VERIFY** table at the end of each reference (IDs like `L-XXX-01`). Present them as what the video claimed, not as current values; none have been re-checked yet.
- `⚠ conflict` / `⚠ unverified` mark open questions in the sources; mention the uncertainty if you rely on one.
- Core macro thinking and the glossary are in the `mlbb-tactics` skill.
