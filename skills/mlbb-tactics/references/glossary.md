# Glossary of MLBB tactical terms
Sources: TERM
Scope: Definitions of the vocabulary the other topic files rely on, in match order (draft → early → mid → late). Definitions only as the video states them. Hero, item and spell names used as examples in the video are dropped (abstraction rule). Numbers and timers are placeholders registered in the LIVE table at the end.

## 1. Damage and hero concepts
- **Burst**: damage that comes out as much and as fast as possible. (src: TERM 00:00)
- **DPS**: damage that comes out bit by bit over time; the opposite of burst. (src: TERM 00:15)
- **Meta**: "the most effective tactic available". Meta heroes are very strong, off-meta heroes are considered very weak. (src: TERM 00:35)
  - IF a hero is very strong in the current meta THEN it must be picked or banned ("pick or ban hero") BECAUSE leaving it open lets the enemy use it. (src: TERM 01:05)
  - The video claims which damage style the meta favours; that claim is time-bound → L-GLOSS-01.
- **Poke**: a hero type good at dealing small amounts of damage to the enemy. (src: TERM 01:26)
- **Sustained**: survives through healing or shields. **Tanky**: survives through health and defence. (src: TERM 01:26–01:44)
- **Initiator**: a hero that starts fights with its skills. "Usually, tanky heroes are also initiators." (src: TERM 02:06) (video's example heroes → dropped; role claims → L-GLOSS-02)
- **Jungler**: a role that uses Retribution and farms the jungle. **Core**: your team's main damage dealer. **Hyper**: a strategy where every resource goes to one person so they can carry the team. (src: TERM 01:05)
- **Heal** restores health; **anti-heal** reduces the enemy's healing; **shield** is temporary protection that absorbs damage. (src: TERM 01:44)
- **Crowd control (CC)**: an effect that hinders enemy movement; it does not affect heroes that are immune. (src: TERM 02:07)
- **Immune vs untargetable** (src: TERM 02:28)
  - Immune: unaffected by damage or CC; you can still be attacked, the attack is just useless. Good for tanks, who still soak attention and damage.
  - Untargetable: enemies cannot attack or target you, so you cannot absorb their damage. Good for squishy heroes (assassins, marksmen).
- **On-hit vs critical** (src: TERM 02:54)
  - On-hit: an effect that enhances basic attacks (e.g. extra damage on every basic attack); on-hit heroes usually rely on attack speed.
  - Critical: chance-based boost to basic-attack damage. Critical damage is the multiplier (100% crit damage = doubled). Critical chance is the likelihood (50% = 1 in 2 attacks); the on-screen example alternates normal and doubled hits. (src: TERM 03:08)
  - TIP: "Criticals are useless if you don't scale correctly." (src: TERM 03:35)
- **Scale**: how much a hero gets stronger from a particular stat. Higher physical attack → more damage for a hero that scales on physical attack; the on-screen numbers are illustrative only → L-GLOSS-03. (src: TERM 03:41)
- **Damage types** (src: TERM 03:53): physical attack → physical damage (red); magic power → magic damage (blue); **adaptive** = physical if physical attack is higher than magic power, magic otherwise; **true damage** = strongest, penetrates all defensive stats (white). → L-GLOSS-04
- **Damage reduction**: you receive less damage. **Physical attack reduction**: an enemy afflicted by it deals less physical damage. (src: TERM 04:16) The card shows only the heading "You'll receive less damage" and an ambiguous 100 vs 80 illustration; the clear 100 → 80 illustration belongs to physical attack reduction. ⚠ unverified: no rule or number for damage reduction itself. → L-GLOSS-04 (damage types) and the card illustrate only.
- **Lifesteal / spell vamp / hybrid lifesteal** (src: TERM 08:50): lifesteal heals from basic-attack damage; spell vamp heals from skill damage; hybrid lifesteal is the strongest and heals from any damage. The video says lifesteal is the best way to survive enemy ganks. → L-GLOSS-08
- **Stacks**: accumulated effects/bonuses that increase with time or actions; the video's clip shows a skill that grants a stack when used to land a last hit. (src: TERM 10:25)

## 2. Draft terms
- **Blind ban** (both teams ban simultaneously; used in public matches) vs **draft ban** (bans alternate; used in professional matches). (src: TERM 04:36) → L-GLOSS-13
- **Top bans** = first 3 bans; **bottom bans** = last 2 bans. (src: TERM 04:51) → L-GLOSS-13
- **Respect ban** (TIP #2): ban a hero not because it is strong but because the enemy player is exceptionally good with it. (src: TERM 05:06) ⚠ unverified: the player/hero pairs named were garbled in the transcript.
- **Draft pick**: teams take turns picking, you see the enemy's picks and may choose the same heroes. **Flex pick**: a hero playable in more than one role/position. **Blind pick**: choosing a lane without knowing the matchup. **Counter pick**: a hero that is very strong against a specific enemy hero, "the most important type of pick". (src: TERM 05:21–05:36)
- **Last pick**: the last pick slot (the "unlucky" slot). You may ask the team to swap pick order, and to justify it you show your win rate (the pick screen shows per-hero matches and win rate). (src: TERM 05:51)
- **Win rate** = Total Win / Total Match × 100%. **Troll**: a player who prevents you from winning, usually on purpose; many trolls depress your win rate. **Lose streak / win streak**: multiple losses / wins in a row. (src: TERM 06:09–06:20)
- **Dodge**: a draft of four marksmen would be dodged (the game does not happen). (src: TERM 06:20) → L-GLOSS-05

## 3. Early game, economy and the lane
- **Early game**: the opening phase while tower plating still exists and siege minions still give extra gold/exp (duration → L-GLOSS-06). (src: TERM 06:48)
- **Micro mechanics** = hero-specific knowledge (skills, matchups, power spikes, scaling, items, damage types, hidden mechanics). **Macro skills** = rotations, farming patterns, objectives, split pushing, teamfights, wave control. The video calls both the most important early-game topics. (src: TERM 06:48)
- **Plating**: the golden layer on a tower; attacking it gives some gold. Other gold sources: jungle monsters and lane minions. (src: TERM 07:10) → L-GLOSS-07
- **Wave**: one group of minions spawned together. **Cutting**: reaching the minions before they get to the lane centre. (src: TERM 07:10)
- **Wave states** once the wave reaches the centre (src: TERM 07:24–07:38; on-screen cards are followed over the audio, see conflict below):
  - **Slow push**: save up minions to build a massive wave by keeping exactly one more minion than the enemy.
  - **Hard push**: clear the enemy minions as fast as possible so your minions are shoved to the enemy tower.
  - **Freeze**: keep the minions where they are by keeping your minion count equal to the enemy's.
  - ⚠ conflict (inside TERM): the audio attributes "build a massive wave" to hard push, the on-screen cards (Versus #9, 07:40–07:52) put it under slow push. The cards are followed here; the frames confirm the card wording.
- **Last hit**: dealing the final blow on a minion to get its gold. **Deny** (TIP #3, "the one thing you must do when farming"): make the enemy lose minions and gold, usually by zoning. **Zoning**: keeping the enemy out of an area (lane, Turtle pit, Lord pit). (src: TERM 07:54–08:08)
- **Solo kill**: killing the enemy laner 1v1. (src: TERM 08:22)
- **KDA**: Kill/Death/Assist ratio showing how often you kill or assist relative to how often you die (kills and assists green, deaths red). Formula, as shown on screen: **(Kill + Assist) / Death**. (src: TERM 08:22, 08:41 — confirmed in the gap-fill from existing frames)
- **Gank**: going to another lane to help kill the enemy; **pathing**: the route you take. IF you expect a gank THEN play safe BECAUSE survival decides the lane. (src: TERM 08:50)
- **Dive**: entering the enemy tower to secure a kill. **Peel**: guarding a teammate from enemy attacks. IF you expect to be dived THEN your roamer should peel for you. (src: TERM 09:18)

## 4. Tempo and snowballing
- **Power spike**: when a hero is at its strongest; can occur in the early, mid or late game. To capitalise on a spike you **invade** (read the enemy jungle and steal camps). (src: TERM 09:46)
- A jungler should know the three buff sources (blue, red, Turtle); with them the team reaches its power spike faster. Their effects → L-GLOSS-09. (src: TERM 09:18)
- **Snowball**: win the early game and use the advantage to grow it. **Bounty**: extra gold the enemy gets for killing someone far ahead. MISTAKE: snowballing carelessly. WHY: your bounty becomes very high and lets the enemy **comeback** (turn a losing game into a winning one). (src: TERM 09:46–10:08)

## 5. Mid game
- **Mid game**: from the end of the early game until the Lord spawns (minutes → L-GLOSS-10). (src: TERM 10:43)
- **Clash / war**: a fight with more than 2 people, some on the front line and some on the back line. The best thing in a clash is to **lock the enemy back line** = focus one specific enemy. (src: TERM 10:43–11:01)
- **Flank**: attacking from the side or back while the enemy is not ready (the enemy can still **kite** = attack while moving). IF you want a flank to succeed THEN use **conceal** (an effect that makes you invisible). Conceal also enables **pick off** (taking enemies out one by one). (src: TERM 11:01)
- **Split push**: pushing the farthest lane to pressure the map. Warning: be careful when attacking, because the enemy can also split push. **Straight push**: the whole team pushes one lane. **3-lane push**: split up to push all lanes; viable only if you have the Lord. (src: TERM 11:30)

## 6. Late game
- **Late game** starts after the Lord spawns; the Lord later evolves (timing → L-GLOSS-11). (src: TERM 11:58)
- **Lord minions**: enhanced minions after defeating the Lord. **Super minions**: appear when you destroy the enemy inhibitor. Lord minions are stronger than super minions. (src: TERM 12:08) → L-GLOSS-12
