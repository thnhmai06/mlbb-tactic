# Emblems: choosing the set by role and sub-type
Sources: EMB (primary), GOLD (emblem decision after the pick), TERM / BUILD (scaling and role concepts)
Scope: The method for choosing an emblem category and its talents from a hero's role, sub-type and needed stats, plus the per-sub-type exception rules. Talent names, hero names and hero lists are removed (abstraction rule): each talent is stated by the effect it provides. The category labels (assassin, tank, fighter, common, mage, support, marksman) are the video's emblem categories, kept only as handles; their existence is a LIVE item (L-EMB-01).
Data quality: the screens were icon-only with no talent captions; the video's talent names came from the narration (error-prone) and the hero portraits are unlabelled. Treat the stat priorities below as the reliable part.

## 1. Core framework
- Emblem choice is NOT tied to hero class: the right set follows what the hero needs (damage type, sustain source, mana, base damage). Wrong versus right emblem on the same items makes a big damage difference and can decide kills, escapes, gold and the game. (src: EMB 00:00, 00:30)
- Do not learn emblems one by one: understand WHICH role/sub-type uses WHICH emblem and talents. (src: EMB 00:30)
- Every set has four slots: emblem category, talent 1, talent 2, core talent (several options may be shown per slot). (src: EMB 00:30, 01:14)
- Method (src: EMB 01:00): (1) identify the hero's role and sub-type, (2) identify the stats that sub-type must **maximise**, (3) choose the emblem and talents that give those stats, (4) check the exception list.
- Compare with the enemy before choosing for a lane: IF you are weaker than the enemy gold laner in the early game THEN use a defensive set; IF even or stronger THEN use the role emblem that fits the hero. (src: GOLD 02:43–03:02; specific option → L-EMB-03)
- Emblem categories in the video's selector: assassin, tank, fighter, common, mage, support, marksman (7). (src: EMB 00:40; L-EMB-01)
- The video rates difficulty per role (assassin easy, tank medium, mage medium, support easy); opinion only. (src: EMB 01:34; L-EMB-02)

- Framing from the video: tanks cannot deal much damage; "if you want to be tanky and deal lots of damage play fighters"; fighters can deal a lot of damage but not as much as mages; mages are strong damage dealers but "nothing without their support". (src: EMB 02:49, 04:20, 05:20)

## 3. Exceptions (5 "pay attention" cases; example heroes → L-EMB-06)
- **#1 tanks** (src: EMB 02:26–02:49): IF the tank has HIGH MANA CONSUMPTION THEN swap the tank emblem for the common emblem. IF the tank needs extra HEAL and MOVEMENT SPEED THEN swap it for the support emblem. "With that you will be tanky."
- **#2 shield-sustain fighter** (src: EMB 03:14): IF the fighter's sustain comes from a SHIELD rather than spell vamp THEN swap the fighter emblem for the support emblem.
- **#3 multi-build and high-base-damage fighters** (src: EMB 03:53, 04:10):
  - IF a hero has MULTIPLE BUILDS THEN for the tank build swap the assassin emblem for the support emblem; for the physical build swap it for the marksman emblem.
  - IF a hero has HIGH BASE DAMAGE and "doesn't need more damage" THEN swap the assassin emblem for the tank emblem and change talent 1 to the damage-thrill-type talent. Rule: when base damage is already high, take defence instead of more damage.
- **#4 high-mana mage** (src: EMB 05:10): swap the mage emblem for the common emblem, for hybrid regen.
- **#5 magic-damage marksmen** (src: EMB 06:13): prefer the mage emblem instead of the marksman emblem.
- Cross-role pattern: mana trouble → common emblem; shield/heal sustain or extra movement speed → support emblem; magic damage on a marksman → mage emblem; high base damage → defence emblem.

## 4. Worked examples
- The same items on two marksmen: one using the assassin emblem and one using the mage emblem, versus a tank using the support emblem: the right set does much more damage; the emblem follows damage type and needs, not class. (src: EMB 00:00)
- A critical assassin uses a critical talent instead of the standard assassin set. (src: EMB 01:16)
- Comparison in lane: weaker early than the enemy gold laner → defensive set; even or stronger → role emblem. (src: GOLD 02:43–03:02)
