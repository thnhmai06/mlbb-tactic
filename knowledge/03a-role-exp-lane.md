# Role: XP (exp) lane
Sources: EXP (primary), with cross-references to MACRO, TERM, DRAFT
Scope: What an XP laner does in draft, the 1v1 early game, minion control, the crab, objective (Turtle) fights, team-fight targeting and late-game map opening. Hero names, timers and levels are removed (abstraction rule); the numbers the rules rely on are in the LIVE table. The video is slide-based and its hero icons are unlabelled, so only audio-level hero claims existed, and those were dropped.

## 1. Draft: which kind of XP laner
- XP heroes split into two types, damage-dealing and tanky; a third group sits between them ("tanky-damage"). Which to pick depends on the rest of your team (and later on the enemy). (src: EXP 00:00, 00:32; the hero lists are in L-EXP-01)
- IF your jungler and roamer are both tanky (2 tanks) THEN pick a damage-dealing XP laner. (src: EXP 00:40)
- IF exactly one of them is tanky THEN pick a tanky-damage hero; do NOT pick a pure damage dealer. (src: EXP 00:40)
- IF neither is tanky (0 tanks) THEN pick a tanky XP laner. (src: EXP 00:40)
- BECAUSE the team needs a front line; add a damage XP laner only when the jungler and roamer already provide the tankiness. (src: EXP 01:00)
- The video shows a full classification slide (damage / tanky-damage / tanky) with unlabelled icons. ⚠ unverified: the row membership was never identified. (src: EXP 01:13)

## 2. Draft: pick order (TIP 1/4)
1. IF an overpowered hero is not banned THEN first-pick it; this overrides the rules below. (src: EXP 01:22)
2. Damage-dealing XP laner: NEVER first pick. BECAUSE your hero has to counter the enemy, so wait and counterpick. (src: EXP 01:22)
3. Adjust your damage type: IF your team already has a lot of magic damage THEN pick a physical hero, and vice versa. (src: EXP 01:39; same rule in GOLD 02:30)
4. Tanky XP laner: you MUST first pick. BECAUSE the tank uses early pick priority so the damage dealers (later picks) can counterpick the enemy. (src: EXP 01:39, 01:55)

## 3. Counterpicking logic
- Counterpicking = choosing a hero specifically to counter the enemy hero. (src: EXP 02:09)
- The video teaches counters as exploiting a weakness by property (src: EXP 02:09–02:44, slide chain "sustain ← true damage ← burst"):
  - a hero that sustains itself from its passive and items is countered by a hero with a lot of true damage;
  - a high-damage hero that is fragile and weak in lane is countered by a burst hero;
  - a burst hero whose skills are all dashes is useless against a hero that stops or counters dashes.
- Specific hero examples for each link → dropped; the mechanism that must still exist → L-EXP-02.

## 4. Early game structure
- The early game (up to the video's "minute 8" convention, L-EXP-03) has laning, first Turtle, laning, second Turtle (the audio says "3 phases" but lists four items). All of it feeds the mid and late game. (src: EXP 02:44)
- FATAL MISTAKE: damaging minions carelessly (breaking wave control). Learn three techniques: fast clear, freeze, cutting a lane. (src: EXP 03:03)

### Fast clear
- Clear all minions as fast as possible. Use it before the Turtle spawns or before rotating so you do not miss minions. DOWNSIDE: it leaves you very vulnerable to a lane freeze, so do not overuse it. (src: EXP 03:17, 03:28)

### Freeze
- Freezing = holding the wave stationary. It is the technique you use the MOST. (src: EXP 03:36)
- Very strong when the wave is on your side: it forces the enemy into a dangerous position. (src: EXP 03:44)
- Mechanic: IF your minions outnumber the enemy's THEN the wave slowly pushes forward; IF the enemy's outnumber yours THEN it moves back; to keep it frozen keep the counts equal. (src: EXP 03:52; same count rule in TERM 07:38)
- Freezing near the ENEMY side is extremely dangerous because you are the one exposed. (src: EXP 04:04)

### Cutting a lane
- Cutting = clearing the enemy minions before they meet yours; purpose: rotate without losing minions. (src: EXP 04:17, 04:25)
- It is extremely dangerous. ALWAYS know where the enemy jungler is before cutting. (src: EXP 04:25)

### Breaking a freeze (mandatory XP-laner skill)
- Two ways: (1) push your minions until the enemy turret clears them so the wave resets to the middle; (2) force the enemy to clear your minions so the wave moves back. (src: EXP 04:39)
- Trick: stand near your cannon minions. IF the enemy attacks you THEN their minions also hit you and over time the wave drifts backward. (src: EXP 04:58)
- Key rule: IF the enemy does not attack you THEN you do not attack them; just stand still and let their minions come. (src: EXP 05:07)

## 5. The crab and winning the 1v1
- The lane crab is worth about one cannon minion; securing it can get you to the level needed for the first objective fight ahead of the enemy XP laner. (src: EXP 05:36; numbers → L-EXP-04)
- Arriving there first puts the enemy in a bind: clear the wave and arrive late, or come under-levelled. (src: EXP 05:45)
- How to win the 1v1: mechanics matter, but the one concept that guarantees it is the POWER SPIKE: heroes are strongest in the early, mid or late game. Understand your matchup; with equal mechanics the right matchup always wins. (src: EXP 05:58)
- Cross-reference: power spike definition in 01-glossary; per-hero spike slides → L-EXP-07.

## 6. Turtle fights (first and second)
- Main job: ZONE THE ENEMY JUNGLER. (src: EXP 06:36)
- Never arrive late; arrive BEFORE your jungler by cutting the wave or fast clearing. (src: EXP 06:44)
- Hide in the bush next to the objective; IF the enemy mid or jungler appears THEN attack immediately. ⚠ unverified: which bush the video points at was not identifiable. (src: EXP 06:44)
- Give the enemy jungler no freedom: harass as aggressively as possible; the goal is to make it hard for the enemy jungler to use Retribution to secure the objective. (src: EXP 06:52; Retribution rule → L-EXP-05)
- Early game = you 1v1 the enemy; in the mid game fights and rotations begin. (src: EXP 07:05)

## 7. Team fights (mid game)
- Build a priority list: rank the enemy damage dealers by who has the most COMPLETED items, focus that one and dump all your skills on them; ignore everything else. BECAUSE your job is to make the enemy damage dealers struggle. (src: EXP 07:12, 07:25)

## 8. Late game: the deciding phase
- Your two main tasks are opening the map and managing minions. (src: EXP 07:41)
- NEVER open the map alone: IF you are caught THEN the enemy instantly takes the Lord and you lose (L-EXP-06). (src: EXP 07:49)
- Memorise the most important bushes to control. ⚠ unverified: the slide showed bush silhouettes only; locations are not identifiable. (src: EXP 08:03)
- Before opening the map, make your minion waves optimal: (src: EXP 08:11)
  - always clear the lane FARTHEST from the Lord first;
  - IF the waves are even THEN do not full-clear; leave 1 cannon minion so the lane slow-pushes. (src: EXP 08:18)
- IF the enemy marksman or mage is offside (out of position or alone) THEN do not hesitate, go all in and eliminate them. (src: EXP 08:27)
- Late-game team fights: enemies hit much harder, so you cannot engage carelessly. Flank from the side and prioritise the enemy marksman. (src: EXP 08:35, 08:41)
