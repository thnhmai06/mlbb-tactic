# How To Mid Lane Macro Like Immortal Players
Source: How To Mid Lane Macro Like Immortal Players.mp4 | ~8:11 | Pro-style mid lane (mage) macro by game phase: draft/mage type, boots, early rotations and turtle setup, mid-game jungler/gold-lane focus, late-game obligations. Narrator says it is derived from watching games of Sanji (Liquid), Yue (Aurora) and "Hijime" (title card shows MPL PH and M7 Grand Finals clips).

## Summary
- Mid laners fall into three mage types: Burst, DPS, Utility. Pick by team composition (enemy tankiness, team magic damage, roamer ability to pick off).
- Early game: clear mid with poke, help jungler with the first neutral camp (heard "Litho Wanderer"), return to clear the wave; rotate to the XP lane only if the hero is mobile; always set up the first turtle a fixed time before spawn.
- Turtle fights: zone, stay behind tank/XP laner, never wait alone in bushes; burst mage flanks to delete enemy jungler and stays hidden on the map until the moment to appear.
- Three big macro mistakes: rotating at the wrong time (wave timing), getting picked off, staying too long in a lane (gold/exp split).
- Mid game: priority shifts from XP laner to jungler (compare levels on the scoreboard), stand on the correct side of the map, then gank the gold lane (easier than XP lane at this stage). Late game: don't get picked off, break through the enemy Wind of Nature (WON), save strongest skills for marksmen; don't push turrets; be first to defend base.

### Early game macro ("Help Exp Laner" slide)
- [02:37] [T] Early rotation order after buying:
  1. [02:41] [T] Clear the mid lane while poking the enemy mid as much as possible.
  2. [02:48] [T] Help your jungler take the first neutral camp (heard "Litho Wanderer"); minimap at 02:48 shows mage and ally near mid with an enemy near mid.
  3. [02:52] [T] Return to mid and clear the next wave quickly.
- [03:02] [T] Then branch by mobility:
  - High-mobility mage (examples in table): rotate directly to the XP lane, gank as much as possible, then return to mid to clear the wave; repeat. (Minimap 03:05 shows mage in the top-left lane with ally and an enemy; the dimmed second minimap at 03:28 is the other branch.)
    - [03:02] [T] Why: if the XP lane is ahead, securing the turtle becomes much easier; goal is to give the XP laner a strong advantage.
  - No fast mobility (examples in table): focus on clearing the mid lane.
    - [03:10] [T] Why: if you miss minions you won't reach the required level for the first turtle (number in table).
- [03:25] [T] Regardless of hero: gank the XP lane and set up the turtle a fixed time before it spawns (number in table).
- [03:38] [T] Minimap at 03:38 (turtle-setup clip) shows mage, ally and enemy stacked at the top-left lane/turtle area with a "26" counter overlay (meaning not stated; probably a countdown).

### Turtle fights and fight position
- [03:40] [T] Your job in turtle fights is to zone enemies. You are not the XP laner.
  - [03:45] [T] Stay behind your tank or XP laner.
  - [03:50] [T] Do not wait alone in the bushes: you'll get picked off.
  - [03:56] [T] Wait, and engage when your XP laner engages.
- [04:00] [T] When a fight starts positioning is everything and the correct position depends on hero type ("Fight Position" slides):
  - DPS or Utility mage: stay in safe bushes, behind walls, or behind your tank (slide shows three example clips: bush, wall, behind tank).
  - Burst mage: flank together with your team; goal is to delete the enemy jungler.
- [04:24] [T] TIP #3/3 (key trick to avoid being picked off): do not show yourself on the map; appear only at the right moment, then burst with your full combo. (Clip shows burst mage ult-ing and "WIPED OUT" on the enemy; minimap in the clip shows the mage hidden until the strike.)
- [04:30] [T] Repeat this until the third turtle is secured.

### The 3 biggest mistakes ("Biggest Mistakes" slide)
1. [04:46] [T] Rotating at the wrong time. (Slide label: "Rotating at the wrong time.")
   - [04:52] [T] Minion waves spawn at fixed seconds each minute and need a fixed walking time from base to center, so waves meet in the center at fixed seconds of the minute (numbers in table; video examples use clock times such as 10:10 and 12:40).
   - [05:06] [T] When rotating, remember this timing and calculate so you don't miss minion waves.
   - [05:02] [T] Minimap clip shows an arrow from the top-left lane toward the bottom-right with numbers on the dashed arrow ("9" at 05:02, "13" at 05:13); meaning not stated (probably rotation travel time).
2. [05:12] [T] Don't get picked off: always take safe paths (minimap clip at 05:16 shows a safe curved path from the lane toward the center) and, once you arrive, don't stay too long.
3. [05:20] [T] Don't stay too long: minions' gold and XP will be split (slide "Don't stay too long"; minimap 05:22 shows mage and ally sharing the top-left lane with an enemy and gold icons).

### Gold spending: anti-heal magic item logic
- [05:24] [T] After the early game you will have a lot of gold but many players misuse it. Ask: do you really need to buy the anti-heal item (glowing wand)?
- [05:34] [T] It is an anti-heal item: only good when the enemy has a lot of healing.
- [05:42] [T] The game already has two other anti-heal items, so decide who buys it:
  - [05:51] [T] Solo play (random teammates) with no anti-heal on the team: you must buy it. (Slide "Solo Play" shows the five roles with heroes and an item column per role; small, see Uncertain.)
  - [05:55] [T] Party play: if you deal area damage, buy the anti-heal item. If you don't deal area damage, don't buy it; ask your tank to get the other anti-heal item (heard "dominance ice") instead.

### Mid game macro
- [06:09] [T] Early game priority = help the XP laner; mid game priority = shift to the jungler.
- [06:12] [T] Open the scoreboard and compare your jungler with the enemy jungler: the higher the level, the stronger the retribution. Never let your jungler fall behind. (Clip shows ally jungler level 8 vs enemy jungler level 9 with retribution damage numbers; see table.)
- [06:25] [T] Mandatory trick for position: if your jungler farms on the red side of the map, be on the right side of the map; if he's on blue, be on the left. (Minimap clips 06:12-06:30 show mage and ally between a jungler and the enemy.)
  - [06:38] [T] Help him when he gets invaded and never let enemies steal his camps.
- [06:44] [T] If your jungler is safe, look at other lanes. In the mid game the gold lane is easier to gank than the XP lane.
  - [06:52] [T] Why: the enemy marksmen don't have their defensive items yet.
  - [06:56] [T] Rotate through the path shown on the minimap (clip 06:56 shows a curved path from mid with an ally toward the gank lane), then immediately return to mid.
- [07:01] [T] Always watch the map and move in sync with your jungler and roamer.

### Late game macro (three obligations, slide "Late Game Macro")
- [07:12] [T] Everything above means nothing if you can't play properly in the late game; understand these three obligations.
1. [07:21] [T] Don't get picked off.
   - Never be alone in a lane; stick with your roamer.
   - [07:29] [T] The biggest mistake is clearing a far lane alone; that's your XP laner's job.
2. [07:29] [T] Break through the enemy's WON (item shown on the slide as "WON"; speech "wind of nature"): you must survive because you are the only one who can break through it.
3. [07:37] [T] Save your strongest skills and target the enemy marksmen.
- [07:44] [T] Don't push turrets: you don't deal turret damage.
- [07:47] [T] But if your base is being pushed, you must be the first to defend: use your skills and range to clear minion waves.

## Terms & definitions
- [00:27] Burst mage: kills fast with a short combo; best vs squishy teams and when the roamer can pick off.
- [00:55] DPS mage: continuous damage over time via spammable skills or basic attacks.
- [01:12] Utility mage: chosen for team support (CC, heal, revive, mobility), not damage.
- [04:00] "Fight Position": where to stand in a fight: DPS/Utility in safe bush/behind wall/behind tank; Burst flanks.
- [04:52] Minion timing: waves spawn at fixed seconds each minute and take a fixed time to walk from base to center; they meet in the middle (numbers in table).
- [07:29] WON: Wind of Nature (shown as "WON" on the late game slide), the enemy protective item the mid mage must break through.

## Worked examples
- [00:27] Draft: enemy mostly tanky and your team lacks magic damage -> DPS mage (consistent damage). Team already full of magic damage -> utility mage to support.
- [01:43] Draft: team has a roamer that picks off -> burst mage is optimal.
- [03:02] Early rotation: with a mobile mage go to the XP lane after clearing mid and helping the jungler; gank, return to mid, repeat; with an immobile mage keep clearing mid to hit the required level for turtle.
- [04:00] Turtle fight: DPS/utility mage waits behind tank/XP laner and engages when the XP laner engages; burst mage flanks to delete the enemy jungler, hidden on the map until the moment of strike.
- [04:52] Wave timing: video's example spawn times are 10:10 and 12:40; add the base-to-center walk time (see table) to know when waves meet; time rotations to arrive for the meet.
- [05:51] Anti-heal buy rule: solo and no anti-heal on team -> buy; party and you deal area damage -> buy; party and no area damage -> tank buys the other anti-heal item.
- [06:12] Mid game scoreboard check: compare your jungler's level with the enemy jungler; if lower, help him by standing on his side (right of map if he is on red side, left if on blue side).
