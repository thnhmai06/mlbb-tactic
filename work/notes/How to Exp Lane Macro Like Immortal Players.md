# How to Exp Lane Macro Like Immortal Players
Source: How to Exp Lane Macro Like Immortal Players.mp4 | ~9:04 | Exp lane macro across four phases (draft, early game to ~min 8, mid game fights, late game): hero choice and counterpicking, minion control, winning 1v1, turtle zoning, team fight targeting, late-game map opening.

## Summary
- The speaker watched many pro/immortal-level exp laner games and condenses exp macro into: Draft, Early Game (1v1 vs the enemy exp laner, until ~min 8), Mid Game (fights and rotations), Late Game (the "deciding phase": open the map, manage minions, flank).
- Draft: pick tanky or damage-dealing exp laner depending on how many tanky heroes (jungler + roamer) your team already has; tanky exp laner picks FIRST, damage-dealing exp laner picks LATE (counterpick).
- Early game: master minion control (fast clear, freeze, cut lane, breaking a freeze), take the crab, win 1v1 via hero power spikes (matchup), and zone the enemy jungler at turtle fights.
- Team fights: build a priority list of enemy damage dealers by completed items and dump all skills on the top target.
- Late game: never open the map alone, control key bushes, set up minion waves first (clear the lane farthest from the lord; leave 1 cannon minion if waves even), punish an offside marksman/mage, flank and prioritize the enemy marksman.
- Note: the video's frame text is mostly icons/animated slides; hero icons are unlabeled, so hero names below come from the speaker's audio only.

## Tactical knowledge

### Draft: what type of exp laner to pick
- [00:00] [T] Exp heroes divide into 2 types: damage-dealing exp laners and tanky exp laners. Which to pick depends on the rest of your team (and later on the enemy). 
- [00:40] [T] Decision rule based on "Number of Tanky Heroes" among your jungler and roamer (on-screen slide shows 2 / 1 / 0, [01:00]-[01:10]):
  - 2 tanky heroes (jungler and roamer both tanky) -> pick a DAMAGE-dealing exp laner.
  - 1 tanky hero -> pick a "Tanky/Tanky-Damage" hero (on-screen label "Pick a Tanky/Tanky-Damage": heroes between tanky and damage). The key line: do NOT pick a pure damage-dealing exp laner.
  - 0 tanky heroes -> pick a TANKY exp laner.
- [01:00] [T] Why: the team needs a front line; only add a damage exp laner when the jungler and roamer already provide the tankiness.
- [01:13] [T] The video shows a "complete list" slide (Pick Order) classifying exp heroes into three rows: Damage Dealing, Tanky-Damage, Tanky. Hero icons only, unlabeled (see Uncertain). Examples named in audio under [L] below.

### Draft: pick order (TIP #1/4)
- [01:22] [T] If there is an OP hero that is not banned, first-pick that hero (takes priority over the rules below).
- [01:22] [T] Damage-dealing exp laner: NEVER first pick. As a damage dealer your hero must counter the enemy, so wait and counterpick (slide: "Never first pick", damage dealer -> Counter -> enemy hero).
- [01:39] [T] Adjust your damage type: if your team already has a lot of magic damage, pick a physical hero, and vice versa (slide "Adjust your damage type ... pick a physical hero, and vice versa").
- [01:39] [T] Tanky exp laner: you MUST first pick (slide "Must first pick").
- [01:55] [T] Why: as the tank you request/use pick priority so your damage dealers (the later picks) can counterpick the enemy.

### Counterpicking (definition and logic)
- [02:09] [T] Counterpicking = choosing a hero specifically to counter the enemy hero.
- [02:09] [T] Counter chain taught as a pattern of weakness-exploiting (hero examples in audio, see Worked examples): sustain heroes (sustain from passive and items) are countered by heroes with lots of true damage; high-damage heroes that are fragile and weak in lane are countered by burst heroes; burst/dash-only heroes are countered by heroes who nullify dashes. On-screen slide labels: Sustain <- True Damage <- Burst ("Counter" arrows) [02:15]-[02:38].
- [02:33] [T] A hero whose skills are all dashes becomes useless against a hero that stops/counters dashes (see Mincetar example).

### Early game structure
- [02:44] [T] Early game (to ~min 8, per [07:41]) has 3 phases: Laning, first turtle, Laning, second turtle (audio says "3 phases" then lists laning, first turtle, laning, second turtle). All affect mid and late game.
- [02:50] [T] A timeline slide shows 00:00 / 02:00 / 04:00. (Turtle first spawns around the 2:00 mark in shown footage: "Turtle Spawned" message at 02:02 on game clock at [06:52]; this is a game-clock reading, not a stated rule.)
- [03:03] [T] Fatal exp-laner mistake: damaging minions carelessly (i.e., hurting wave control). Three minion-control techniques must be understood: fast clear, freeze, cutting lane (slide "Minion Control" with three boxes: Fast Clear, (freeze), Cutting Lane).

#### Fast clear
- [03:17] [T] Fast clearing = clearing all minions as quickly as possible.
- [03:17] [T] Use it before turtle spawn or before rotating so you do not miss minions.
- [03:28] [T] Downside: makes you very vulnerable to lane freezes, so do not use it too often.

#### Freeze
- [03:36] [T] Freezing = keeping the minion wave stationary in one spot. This is the technique you will use the MOST.
- [03:44] [T] Very strong when the wave is close to you (your side): forces the enemy into a dangerous position.
- [03:52] [T] Mechanic: if your minions outnumber the enemy's, the wave slowly pushes forward; if enemy minions outnumber yours, the wave moves backward. To keep it frozen, keep both sides' minion counts equal.
- [04:04] [T] Freezing is extremely dangerous when the wave is near the ENEMY side (you are the one exposed). How to break a freeze: see below.

#### Cutting a lane
- [04:17] [T] Cutting a lane = clearing the enemy minions before they meet yours (slide at [04:25]).
- [04:25] [T] Purpose: lets you rotate without losing minions.
- [04:25] [T] Extremely dangerous; never do it carelessly. ALWAYS know the enemy jungler's location before cutting.

#### Breaking a freeze (mandatory exp-laner skill)
- [04:39] [T] Two ways: (1) push your minions until the enemy turret clears them so the wave resets to the middle; or (2) force the enemy to clear your minions so the wave moves back.
- [04:58] [T] Lesser-known trick: stand near your cannon minions. If the enemy attacks you, their minions also hit you (the enemy minions are drawn to you); over time the wave moves backward.
- [05:07] [T] Key rule: if the enemy does not attack you, you do not attack them. Just stand still and let the enemy minions come ("enjoy the enemy minions").

### The crab and winning 1v1
- [05:28] [T] In lane, besides the enemy hero, there is the crab. Slide: "How important?" (crab icon) and "1 VS 1".
- [05:36] [T] One crab = one cannon minion in value. If you secure it you can reach level 4 faster than the enemy exp laner.
- [05:45] [T] Reaching level 4 first lets you arrive at turtle earlier than your opponent. The enemy is forced into two bad options: clear the minions and arrive late, or come to turtle at level 3.
- [05:58] [T] How to win 1v1: the key is mechanics, but the "one key concept that guarantees" winning 1v1 is POWER SPIKE: some heroes are strong in the early, mid or late game. Understand the matchup between your hero and the enemy; with equal mechanics the player with the right matchup always wins. (Slide "Hero Power": Early Game column plus two further columns of icons, [06:20].)

### Turtle fights (first and second turtle)
- [06:36] [T] Main job at turtle fights: ZONE THE ENEMY JUNGLER.
- [06:44] [T] Never arrive late. Get there BEFORE your jungler by cutting the wave or fast clearing.
- [06:44] [T] Hide in "this bush" (the speaker points at a bush near the turtle on the minimap slide, [06:40]-[06:47]; exact bush not identifiable from frames, see Uncertain). If the enemy mid or jungler appears, attack them immediately.
- [06:52] [T] Do not give the enemy jungler any freedom: harass as aggressively as possible. Main goal: make it hard for the enemy jungler to use retribution (i.e., to secure the turtle).
- [07:05] [T] Early game = you 1v1 the enemy; mid game is different: fights and rotations begin ("1 VS 1 the enemy" vs "Fights & rotations" on the slide, [07:15]).

### Team fights (mid game)
- [07:12] [T] Prepare a priority list. Look at the enemy damage dealers and rank them by who has the most COMPLETED items. Focus that target and dump all your skills on them; ignore everything else.
- [07:25] [T] Why: your job is to make sure the enemy damage dealers struggle.

### Late game ("deciding phase", from ~min 8)
- [07:41] [T] "By now you understand how to play up until minute 8." The late game is the deciding phase. Your main tasks: opening the map and managing minions. Questions the video answers: which parts of the map to open, which bushes matter, how to manage minions.
- [07:49] [T] Opening the map: NEVER do it alone. If you get caught, the enemy will instantly take the lord and you lose the game.
- [08:03] [T] Memorize the most important bushes to control (slide "Late Game Macro", minimap; highlighted bush shapes at [08:05]-[08:10]; see Uncertain for precise locations).
- [08:11] [T] Before opening the map, always make sure your minion waves are optimal.
- [08:11] [T] Always clear the lane FARTHEST from the lord first.
- [08:18] [T] If the minion waves are even, do not do a full clear: leave 1 cannon minion so the lane slow-pushes. (Slide at [08:12]-[08:20] shows 3 blue vs 3 red minion icons on the exp-lane side, and the lord/turtle pits on the minimap.)
- [08:27] [T] If the enemy marksman or mage is offside (out of position/alone), do not hesitate: go all in and eliminate them.
- [08:35] [T] Late-game team fight difference: enemies hit much harder, so you cannot engage carelessly.
- [08:41] [T] Flank from the side and prioritize the enemy marksman (slide at [08:35]-[08:42] shows exp laner away from the 4 enemy heroes near the lord pit, i.e. on the flank).

## Terms & definitions
- [00:32] Damage-dealing exp laner / tanky exp laner: the two exp hero types. Third group "Tanky-Damage": heroes between tanky and damage (slide label "Tanky - Damage").
- [02:09] Counterpicking: choosing a hero specifically to counter the enemy hero.
- [03:17] Fast clear: clearing all minions as quickly as possible.
- [03:36] Freeze: keeping the minion wave stationary in one spot (equal minion counts both sides).
- [04:17] Cutting a lane: clearing enemy minions before they meet yours.

- [07:12] Priority list: ranking enemy damage dealers by number of completed items to pick the focus target.
- [07:49] Opening the map: moving out to vision-control/clear bushes and take objectives on the map in late game.

## Worked examples
- Counter chain (draft) [02:09]-[02:44]: enemy Uranus (strong from passive and item sustain) -> pick a heavy true-damage hero such as X-Borg; X-Borg (high damage but extremely vulnerable to burst and very weak in lane) -> pick a burst hero such as Benedetta; Benedetta (strong burst and mobility, but ALL skills are dashes) -> becomes useless against Mincetar.
- Team has 0 tanks (jungler and roamer not tanky) -> exp lane picks tanky (e.g., Uranus, Alice, Terizla per audio) and picks first; with 2 tanks -> pick a damage exp laner (e.g., Benedetta, Guinevere, Arlot) and pick late to counter.
- Early lane [05:36]-[05:58]: you secure the crab (=1 cannon minion exp) -> level 4 before enemy -> arrive at turtle first -> enemy must either clear the wave and be late, or arrive at level 3.
- Breaking a freeze [04:58]-[05:28]: wave frozen on your side; stand near your cannon minions; if the enemy hero attacks you, their minions also hit you and the wave drifts back; if the enemy does not attack, stay still.
- Late-game wave setup [08:11]-[08:27]: waves even -> leave 1 cannon minion so lane slow-pushes; clear the lane farthest from the lord first; then open the map with teammates.
