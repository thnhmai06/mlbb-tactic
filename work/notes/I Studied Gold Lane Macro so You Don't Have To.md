# I Studied Gold Lane Macro so You Don't Have To
Source: I Studied Gold Lane Macro so You Don't Have To.mp4 | ~9:45 | Beginner-oriented gold lane macro guide in four phases (draft, early, mid, late game), built from watching pro games (Oheb, Skyler, Kelra); every rule depends on the hero's power spike.

## Summary
- Structure on screen: Draft -> Early Game (to about minute 5) -> Mid Game -> Late Game, plus 5 numbered TIPs and 4 numbered MISTAKEs. [00:00] [T]
- Gold lane macro = adjust the draft, find the best farming pattern per phase, survive 4-man situations, stay alive in teamfights, avoid common gold-laner mistakes; all adjusted by the hero used. [00:15] [T]
- Draft: in 5-man party pick by team composition (poke / front-to-back / dive); in solo queue it depends on rank. Think "1 vs 9": the hero must carry alone. [00:45] [T]
- Early game: farm and harass, read the minimap (safe/neutral/dangerous zones, track enemy mid/jungler/roamer), choose fight vs flight by early power spike, avoid defending turret/overextending, mostly skip first turtle, conditionally help second turtle. [03:50] [T]
- Mid game: still maximize gold; split push or farm a lane (prefer mid lane); help third turtle with strict rules. [07:33] [T]
- Late game: clear pushing waves, never walk into fog alone, survive until teamfight, deal consistent damage from safe position. [08:45] [T]

## Tactical knowledge

### Draft - 5-man party (pick by team composition)
- Rule: in a full 5-man party pick based on team composition; teammates may not understand the theory, so "just follow this guide". [00:55] [T]
- On-screen "Team Composition" table (three comp types and what the gold laner should be): [01:05-01:45] [T]
  - Team has a lot of ranged heroes -> "Poke" camp (comp) -> pick gold laners with "Long Range". [01:08] [T]
  - Team has two or three tanky heroes -> "Front to Back" comp -> pick gold laners with "High DPS". [01:13] [T]
  - Team has dive-type heroes -> "Dive" comp -> pick "Mobile" gold laners. [01:25] [T]

### Draft - not in a 5-man party (depends on rank)
- If Mythic (on-screen text: "Mythic 80 and above"; whisper heard "Mythic AD") or above: fill your teammates' weaknesses using the composition theory above. [01:39] [T]
  - Your gold laner "doesn't have to be a marksman or mage" at this rank. [02:15] [T]
  - Don't pick heroes that rely heavily on communication (on-screen "Mistake #1/4"). Examples named: Zuxin (heard), the Freya-Digi combo, the Hi-Loss-Ixia combo. [01:46] [T]
  - Worked example "High Rank Draft" -> see Worked examples. [02:00] [T]
- If below that rank: pick a hero that can carry. [02:27] [T]
- Damage-type balance: it is not just about your damage type; when the team already has a lot of magic heroes, pick physical and vice versa. [02:30] [T]
- Mindset: "think of the game as 1 vs 9", so make sure your hero can carry on its own. [02:36] [T]

### Emblem choice after picking (compare to enemy gold laner)
- After you pick, immediately check the enemy gold laner and compare it to yours to choose the emblem; two possibilities: [02:43] [T]
  - You are weaker in the early game -> defensive emblem (screen label "Defensive"). [02:53] [T]
  - Even or stronger early game -> use the role emblem that fits the hero (marksman, assassin or mage emblem). [03:02] [T]

### Power spike (hero strength by phase)
- All the above depends on the hero's power spike. Gold laners are strong in early, mid or late game. - Why it matters: the power spike decides what you do in early lane (fight/flight), in turtle decisions, and in mid game lane choice. [06:55] [T]

### Early game (up to about minute 5): core job
- Job: farm while harassing your lane opponent. [03:49] [T]
- Lane zones: there are safe, neutral and dangerous zones (on-screen "Map Zones": 3D lane overlay colored green / yellow / red, with the minimap next to it showing hero in top-left gold lane corner and enemies in the middle; green = side near own turret/base, yellow = middle, red = enemy side. The color-to-name mapping follows the order spoken: safe, neutral, dangerous). [03:58] [T]
- Always check the minimap and keep track of the enemy mid, roamer and jungler. [04:02] [T]
  - If one of them disappears -> move to the neutral zone. [04:08] [T]
  - If two disappear -> fall back to the safe zone. [04:12] [T]
- Common mistake #2: joining "lethal wanderer" (as heard) fights. That is your mid, jungler and roamer's job, not yours; just focus on farming and harassing your lane. (Screen "Mistake #2/4" shows the gold laner icon in the middle of the map.) [04:15] [T]

### Fight or flight (1v1 against lane opponent) - "Fight Vs Flight" TIP #3/5
- The most important thing in the 1v1 is knowing when to fight or flee; three conditions (screen: square = stronger early, circle = weaker early, triangle = even). [04:30] [T]
- Stronger in early game: [04:38] [T]
  - Freeze the minions and zone your opponent so they can't reach the XP area (the area where they gain gold and XP when your minions die). [04:44] [T]
  - By zoning them out, they get low gold and XP. [04:53] [T]
  - Don't overextend; always check enemy mid, jungler and roamer position and keep good positioning. [04:59] [T]
- Weaker in early game (disadvantage): [05:05] [T]
  - Play safe; farm as much as you can in the safe zone. [05:12] [T]
  - "It's better to miss a last hit than die." [05:15] [T]
- Even: [05:20] [T]
  - First farm and poke the enemy as much as possible. [05:20] [T]
  - If enemy mid, jungler or roamer is visible (not missing) and your opponent's HP is low, go for the kill. [05:24] [T]
  - If the lane stays even, wait for your team to gank (on-screen caption "Wait for your team to gank"). [05:32] [T]
  - The key to a successful gank: freeze the wave near your turret. [05:36] [T]
  - On-screen caption "Losing Position" shows enemy hero and enemy minions pressing up to your turret while your hero is by your own minions; implied cue that you are losing lane position. [05:28] [T]

### Surviving the 4-man gank / defending
- Your hero doesn't matter if you are ganked by 4 people; watch the minimap. [05:46] [T]
- If enemy mid, jungler and roamer disappear for too long -> fall back to your inner turret. [05:52] [T]
- Biggest mistake (Mistake #3/4): trying to defend your turret (frame shows hero recalling after turret destroyed). Let them take the plating or even the turret itself, as long as you stay alive. [06:01] [T]

### First turtle (early game)
- Same logic as turret: you don't need to join the fight; stay safe in lane (minimap: teammates grouped at the turtle at bottom-right, gold laner stays in his lane top-left). [06:11] [T]
- Only help the first turtle when you can participate without missing minions (TIP #4/5), e.g. when Chip teleports you there, or when playing Moskov and his ult can't be used (heard as "can't use his ult"; see Uncertain). [06:14-06:27] [T]

### Second turtle (early game)
- It can spawn in either gold lane or XP lane. [06:27] [T]
- If it spawns in gold lane: you must help. [06:37] [T]
  - So don't die before the turtle spawns (screen: Second Turtle minimap, turtle top-left in gold lane with your hero in lane and enemy minions near). [06:40] [T]
  - Clear your minions and move safely toward it. [06:43] [T]
  - Your job: deal as much damage as possible to the turtle or enemies while staying alive. [06:48] [T]
- If it spawns in XP lane: use the power spike guide. [06:55] [T]
  - Hero strong early: can help, but only after pushing your wave (frame at 07:00 shows early-game hero heading to the turtle in the lower right, with the cleared wave shown as cyan dots top-left). [06:57] [T]
  - If the enemy zones you so you can't push: skip the turtle and just farm. [07:01] [T]
  - Hero strong late game: don't help with the turtle; just push your lane instead (frame at 07:10 shows late-game hero staying in gold lane). [07:07] [T]
- Fatal mistake (Mistake #4/4): overextending and getting solo-killed; if your team then loses the turtle they tilt and the game goes very bad. Priority is to stay alive. [07:15] [T]
  - Frame: enemy 4 near the turtle, 4 allies approaching, one more enemy hero on the right; gold laner not safely positioned. [07:10] [T]

### Mid game
- Goal stays the same as early game: get as much gold as possible, but now you rotate. (Goal slide shows hero icon with "+ 100 Gold"; meaning of 100 not explained, see Uncertain.) [07:33] [T]
- Two options [07:41] [T]
  - If hero has mobility (Moskov, Cloud, Wanwan as examples): split push outer turrets that are still standing (screen "Split Push" vs "Farming" split view; minimap shows the hero going to a far outer turret at the bottom-right corner circled in red while teammates group at the blue base/middle). [07:49] [T]
  - If not possible: farm in lane. [07:57] [T]
- Which lane to farm: best is mid lane, because minions reach the center faster; if your mid laner doesn't let you, pick whichever lane still has its outer turret intact. [08:01-08:13] [T]
- Third turtle: you must help take it. TIP #5/5 rules (on-screen list): [08:17] [T]
  - Don't hit the turtle without your jungler (screen text; whisper misheard as "younger"). [08:21] [T]
  - Don't show up on the map before the turtle spawns. [08:27] [T]
  - Don't stay in a far lane before the fight. [08:29] [T]
  - Don't take the buff when your team is already ready for war (spoken only, not on the list slide). [08:31] [T]

### Late game
- Gold lane macro in late game is simple: clear waves on pushing lanes and catch enemy minion waves. (On-screen "Late Game Macro" minimap with red enemy minion dots pushing the gold lane at left and cyan allied dots in center/bottom; the gold laner is at/near own base in several frames.) [08:45] [T]
- Most important rule: never walk into the fog of war alone; you'll get ambushed; always stay close to your team. [08:57] [T]
- Teamfight job (3-point on-screen list "Late Game Macro"): [09:01-09:24] [T]
  1. Survive until the teamfight, because that's when you shine. [09:01] [T]
  2. Don't dive or pick off enemies; deal as much consistent damage as possible from a safe position, even if it's hitting tanks or fighters. [09:06] [T]
  3. If you see a chance to target enemy damage dealers, capitalize on it. [09:19] [T]

## Terms & definitions
- Poke camp / comp: team with a lot of ranged heroes; gold laner should have long range. [01:08] [T]
- Front-to-back camp/comp: team with 2-3 tanky heroes; gold laner should have high DPS. [01:13] [T]
- Dive camp/comp: team with dive-type heroes; gold laner should be mobile. [01:25] [T]
- Power spike: the phase (early/mid/late) at which a hero is strongest; shown as early (red) / mid (blue) / late (green) groups. [03:15] [T]
- XP area: the area where you gain gold and XP when your minions die (zoning the enemy out of it starves them). [04:47] [T]
- Safe / neutral / dangerous zone: lane areas in the "Map Zones" overlay (green, yellow, red as shown); used with the minimap rule (1 enemy missing -> neutral; 2 missing -> safe). [03:58] [T]
- Freeze: holding the minion wave in place (here near your turret for ganks, or to zone the enemy). [04:40] [T]
- Inner turret: the turret closest to your base in lane, where you fall back to when enemies vanish too long. [05:52] [T]
- Plating: turret plating, which the enemy may take instead of you losing your life. [06:03] [T]
- Split push: pushing a far outer turret alone (map pin icon), vs "Farming". [07:49] [T]
- 1 vs 9: mindset that your gold laner must carry on his own. [02:36] [T]
