# How Pro Players Improvise Items
Source: How Pro Players Improvise Items.mp4 | ~8:30 | Nine "tips" on how to adapt your item build mid-game: pick the right counter/variant item by the enemy's stats and damage type, buy cheap situational items early, and use "very specific" items only in the right conditions.

## Summary
- Improvising items = swapping/choosing items according to (a) your hero's damage source (skill vs basic attack, burst vs DPS) and (b) the enemy's defense, healing and damage type.
- Key principle (stated on screen, Tip 5/9): "The key to improvising items is how fast you can counter the enemy". Finished items are expensive and slow to buy, so learn the CHEAPER components/items that give one needed function (shield, penetration, attack speed, mana, cooldown, spell vamp, movement speed) and buy them early.
- Defensive improvising is called "more important" than offensive improvising: pick the defensive item by the enemy's damage TYPE and delivery (skill / attack speed / critical / burst magic / DPS magic).
- Final tip: do not auto-replace an almost-finished Immortal with Winter Crown; check your cooldowns (Retribution/escape) first.
- Video structure on screen: "Versus #1/4 - #4/4" (item vs item), "Tip #1/9 - #9/9", "Cheaper Physical Items", "Cheaper Magic Items", "Defensive Items", "Very Specific".

## Terms & definitions
- Improvise items: adjust item choices to the situation, mid-game. [T]
- Finished item: full-price, expensive item you need much time to buy; cheaper components are the quick counters. [02:14] [T]
- Unique passive: item passive that does not stack with another item having the same passive (Malefic Gun and Malefic Roar both give 30% physical penetration). [00:21] [T]
- Skill heroes: heroes whose physical damage comes from their skills. Basic-attack heroes: damage comes from basic attacks; subdivided into Burst, and DPS (Critical / Attack Speed). [00:30-01:32] [T]
- Burst: delivers its damage in one quick combination (physical or magic) -> countered by shield items (Athena's Shield for magic burst). DPS: sustained damage over time (magic: counter with Radiant Armor). [06:40-06:50] [T]
- "Very Specific" items: must-haves only under named conditions (Oracle, Blood Wings, Wind of Nature, Immortal). [06:55] [T]
- Scoreboard / Attributes tab: in-game screen listing each hero's attack, power and defenses; not fully reliable for enemy magic defense because of activated items. [04:02] [T]

## Worked examples
- Choosing a penetration item: skill physical hero -> Malefic Roar (Gun's attack speed/range wasted) [00:34].
- DPS crit hero vs high-armor target: check the enemy's physical defense against the threshold; above it, Roar, otherwise Gun [01:08].
- Versus healers (Angela's Heal shown on screen): pen items do not help; buy Sea Halberd (physical) or Glowing Wand (magic) [01:52, 04:20].
- Squishy physical hero build: boots, finished item, Magic Blade, finished item, ..., Rose Gold Meteor only after three finished items [02:30-02:50].
- Burst skill hero: Fury Hammer before the first finished item, later upgrade to Heptaseas (more burst) or Hunter Strike (more movement speed) [02:56-03:17].
- Attack-speed hero facing tanks: boots -> Swift Crossbow -> DHS before upgrading to Corrosion Scythe [03:20-03:38].
- Mana-hungry mage: two Magic Necklaces early if building Azure Blade/Demon Shoes; Mystery Codex then one Magic Necklace if building Enchanted Talisman [04:50-05:10].
- Objective steal defence: Joy sells Immortal mid-animation, takes Rose Gold Meteor because Retribution is ready, uses Retribution and wins [08:04-08:28].
