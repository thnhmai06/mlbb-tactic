# High Rank Chat Codes Explained in MLBB
Source: High Rank Chat Codes Explained in MLBB.mp4 | ~2:38 | Short explainer of the abbreviations/short words used in high-rank MLBB team chat and what action each one asks of you. The narrator does not read the codes aloud; they were transcribed from on-screen chat lines and overlay labels (frames viewed about every 1-3 s).

## Summary
- High-rank team chat is not random typing: it is fast, short-form calls. Each call is "keyword (+ hero/role or lane)" and asks for a specific action. [02:27] [T]
- Call families shown: lane control (freeze), enemy battle-spell/skill tracking (F / P / "750 exp" / "425 mage" / ns), enemy hiding spot (lbm / rbm), protect (peel), split/rotate (seg), flank/dive (flnk), pincer (sandwich), team priority (hero / lord / turt). [T]
- Closing message: one short message can save the whole game. [02:35] [T]

## Tactical knowledge

### Lane control
- Code `freeze` (shown as `freeze top`, `freeze bot`, plain `freeze`; overlay "FREEZE = FREEZE LANE"): means do not touch the minions, do not clear the wave, leave the wave alone. [00:16-00:24] [T]
  - The lane keyword after `freeze` (top / bot) says which lane to freeze; bare `freeze` has no lane. [00:19] [T]

### Enemy battle spell tracking (spell + hero)
- Format `F MM` = "(SPELL + HERO)". Overlay translation: `F MM` = FLICKER MM (marksman); `P MAGE` = PURIFY MAGE. [00:24-00:35] [T]
  - Meaning: the enemy used that battle spell, so that hero (marksman or mage) has no escape / no spell available. [00:33] [T]
  - Action: this is your green light to punish (attack / go in on) that hero. [00:33-00:38] [T]
  - The first letter is the spell (F = Flicker, P = Purify as shown); the second word is the enemy hero/role (MM = marksman as the narrator reads it, mage). [00:30-00:36] [T]
- Format `750 exp` = "(TIME OF AVAILABILITY + SPELL)" (the 750 is a game-clock time; the narrator reads it as 7:50). Meaning: the EXP laner's spell will be available again at 7:50. [00:38-00:43] [T]
  - How to compute the time yourself: time the enemy used the spell + the spell's cooldown. Example in the video: EXP used Flicker at 6:20, add 2 minutes, so type `820 EXP`. [00:44-00:52] [T]
  - Format is: time available (as digits, no colon) + role/hero. `820 EXP` is typed that way in chat. [00:52] [T]
- Format `425 mage` (shown as `425 mage`): the mage's spell will be available at 4:25. Same pattern: time of availability + role. [00:52-00:59] [T]

### Enemy used important skill / ultimate (NS)
- Code `NS` (overlay "NS MM = (NO SKILL + HERO)"; seen as `ns mid` and `ns mage`): means the enemy has used an important skill or their ultimate (the named hero is out of that skill). [00:56-01:08] [T]
  - Action: when you see this call, do not wait; this is your chance to fight. (Narrator joke: "don't wait 5 business days".) [01:02-01:08] [T]

### Enemy location in the mid bushes
- Code `LBM` = Left Bush Mid; `RBM` = Right Bush Mid. Overlay: "LBM RBM = LOCATION OF ENEMY ON BUSH". Seen as `lbm roam` and `rbm mm`. [01:08-01:18] [T]
  - The word after the bush code says WHO is there: `rbm mm` = the enemy marksman is hiding in the right mid bush. [01:15-01:22] [T]
  - Action: do not face-check that bush (do not walk in blindly to check if it is empty). Narrator: "like you're checking if the grass is soft". [01:22-01:28] [T]

### Protect / peel
- Code `peel` (overlay "PEEL = PROTECT"): protect. [01:28-01:34] [T]
  - Meaning: stop chasing the tank with 1 HP and protect the teammate who actually needs help (overlay: "PROTECT IMPORTANT TEAMMATE"). [01:33-01:40] [T]
  - Decision rule: prioritize protecting the important teammate over finishing a low-HP tank. [01:33-01:40] [T]

### Split push / pressure elsewhere
- Code `seg` (overlay "SEG = SEGUE"), seen as `seg top`: if everyone is fighting in one lane (example: fighting bottom), someone calling `seg top` means push top while the enemies are distracted. [01:36-01:44] [T]
  - Reasoning: you are not avoiding the fight; you are creating pressure somewhere else. [01:44-01:52] [T]
  - The lane after `seg` says where to go (top in the example). [01:41] [T]

### Flank / dive the backline
- Code `flnk` (seen as `flnk mm` and `flnk mage`; overlay "DIVE BACKLINE HEROES"): go behind the enemy to reach the backline. [01:52-02:00] [T]
  - The word after `flnk` is the target. If someone says `flnk mage`, your target is the mage, not the tank standing in front ("like a refrigerator"). [02:00-02:07] [T]

### Pincer / sandwich
- Code `sandwich` (overlay "SANDWICH = PRESS IN THE MIDDLE"): if the enemy marksman is alone, you and your teammate go left and right and collapse on it from both sides, so the marksman is caught in the middle. [02:00-02:10] [T]
  - Minimap illustration: two allies approach the isolated enemy marksman (at the bottom-right) from two sides. [02:03-02:10] [T]

### Team priority (target / objective)
- Overlay "TEAM PRIORITY" groups three one-word calls: `hero`, `lord`, `turt`. [02:10-02:17] [T]
- `hero`: focus on killing the enemy hero first; everyone dumps damage on that target; no random side quests. Overlay: "FOCUS ON KILLING ENEMY HERO". [02:17-02:27] [T]
- `lord` or `turt` (Turtle): secure the objective; stop chasing someone who can't be yours (i.e. do not chase a fleeing hero). Overlay: "FOCUS ON SECURING OBJ". [02:22-02:30] [T]
