# How Pro Players Create Their Own Build
Source: How Pro Players Create Their Own Build.mp4 | ~9:24 | Framework for deriving CORE items from a hero's damage source (physical basic-attack crit / attack speed, physical skill sustain / burst, magic DPS / control / burst / sustain), counter-item choices for defensive tanks, and the "scaling" check behind odd builds. Core items only; the video promises a "part two".

## Summary
- A build has two parts: core items (bought every game regardless of either team) and situational items (bought for specific conditions). This video only teaches how to find the CORE items. [00:30] [T]
- Core items come from classifying the hero's DAMAGE SOURCE in a tree: Physical (Basic Attack: Critical / Attack Speed; Skill: Sustain / Burst, where Burst splits into Purely Skills / Weave in Basic Attack) and Magic (DPS / Control / Burst / Sustain). Each leaf needs a different kind of item (crit stats, on-hit, cooldown+sustain, penetration, etc.). [06:36] [T]
- Defensive tanks have no fixed core: every item is situational, chosen from the enemy's main damage source and the heroes you will face most. A counter list (damage source -> defence item -> upgrade) is given. [06:48] [T]
- Offensive tanks have two options (fight often / stay close to enemies); the rest is situational. [08:00] [T]
- The "secret" behind weird builds (tank Lancelot, magic-damage Bane, sustain Julian, healing-item Lolita) is hero SCALING: check what stat each skill scales with and build that stat. [08:30] [T]
- Item names, hero lists and exact orders are in the final table; the tactical sections give the rules as functions/categories.

## Tactical knowledge

### Core vs situational items
- Core items: items you should buy in every single game, no matter what your team or the enemy is doing. On-screen slide "Core Items vs Situational Items" states: "Items you should buy in every single game, no matter what your team or the enemy is doing". [00:45] [T]
- Situational items: items bought based on specific conditions (slide text: "Items bought based on ..."; narration: specific conditions). [00:45] [T]
- Pros' odd builds (e.g. a tank-type hero buying healing items) come from building what the hero's skills scale with, not the hero's class label. [00:00] [T]
- This video covers only core items ("yes, just the core items"). [09:12] [T]

### Step 1: damage type, then damage source
- Two damage types: physical and magic. [01:00] [T]
- For a physical hero ask first: does your hero deal damage through basic attacks or through skills? [01:07] [T]
- Tip #1/5 (on screen): if your skills boost your basic attacks, then you are playing a basic attack hero. [01:15] [T]
- Damage-source tree (on screen, repeated at 04:58 and 06:32): [04:58] [T]
  - Physical -> Basic Attack -> Critical | Attack Speed
  - Physical -> Skill -> Sustain | Burst -> (Purely Skills | Weave in Basic Attack)
  - Magic -> DPS | Control | Burst | Sustain
- That is 8 damage-source types: Physical Basic Attack Critical; Physical Basic Attack Attack Speed; Physical Skill Burst; Physical Skill Sustain; Magic DPS; Magic Control; Magic Burst; Magic Sustain. [06:48] [T]

### Physical basic attack: CRITICAL heroes
- Need items that give critical chance and critical damage (on screen: "Critical chance / Critical damage"). [01:23] [T]
- Of the four crit items in the game, the narration says some are must-haves and the order matters; the order logic: [01:30] [T]
  - The crit item that gives sustain should be your SECOND item because it gives you sustain in the early game. [01:38] [T]
  - Goal = farm fast: buy the crit attack-speed item (named "Windtalker" in the narration) third and the big crit-damage item ("Berserker's") fourth. On screen the label is "Farm fast:". [01:45] [T]
  - Ahead and want to focus on team fights: buy the big crit-damage item third and buy the crit attack-speed item only when needed; it can be fourth, fifth, or even in place of your boots. On screen the label is "Team Fights:" and the third slot is highlighted. [01:53] [T]
- Tip #2/5 (on screen): a further crit item gives movement speed while your ult is active (on-screen item name spelled "Great Dragon Sprear"), so it works well on heroes whose ult is a mobility window. [02:05] [T]

### Physical basic attack: ATTACK SPEED heroes
- Need attack speed and ON-HIT items. [02:15] [T]
- Tip #3/5 "What does On Hit actually mean?": an on-hit item gives an extra effect every time your basic attack hits. [02:23] [T]
  - On-screen examples of on-hit effects: slows enemies; deals bonus damage based on the enemy's HP; triples all on-hit effects. [02:30] [T]
- All three on-hit items are mandatory for attack-speed heroes, but ORDER matters: [02:39] [T]
  - The "triples all on-hit effects" item should ALWAYS be the last item, to maximise its passive (on screen it sits in the 4th slot, after boots + the other two). [02:46] [T]
  - If the enemy has a lot of HP (on screen: "Fighter Gold laner & Tank Roamer"): buy the %-enemy-HP bonus-damage item first. [02:54] [T]
  - If not, buy the slowing item first. [03:01] [T]

### Physical SKILL heroes: SUSTAIN
- Need cooldown reduction plus sustain (on screen: "Cooldown reduction / Sustain"). [03:14] [T]
- Four items shown: a cooldown-reduction belt-type item (stacks), a sustain axe-type item, a wings-type item, and an item that boosts the axe's healing. [03:22] [T]
- Order depends on how much the hero brawls early: [03:25] [T]
  - Hero fights a lot in lane (on-screen label "Fights a lot in lane", three heroes shown): buy the stacking cooldown item SECOND to maximise its stacks, then the sustain item for sustain, then the item that boosts the sustain item's healing. [03:25] [T]
  - If you are still getting bursted: swap the sustain item for the wings item (burst protection). [03:40] [T]
  - Junglers or heroes that rarely brawl early (on-screen label "Jungler / Rarely brawl early", three heroes shown): go with the sustain item's healing; the sustain item goes second (it is highlighted second after boots) and the stacking item is not prioritised. [03:47] [T]

### Physical SKILL heroes: BURST
- Question: does the hero deal damage purely through skills or also weave in basic attacks? [04:06] [T]
- Purely skills (on-screen title "Skill Burst Purely Skills"): [04:14] [T]
  - Start with the penetration item (the screen shows penetration boots as slot 1 and a second item in slot 2). [04:14] [T]
  - If you are snowballing and your team does not have a "Sky Piercer", pick it up next (on-screen label "Snowball & Your Team Doesn't Have a Sky Piercer:"). [04:18] [T]
  - Then upgrade the penetration item (Fury Hammer) into Hunter Strike and finish with Blade of Despair. [04:22] [T]
- Weave in basic attacks (on-screen title "Skill Burst Weave In Basic Attack"): [04:30] [T]
  - The build stays mostly the same with ONE addition: an item that gives bonus burst damage to your basic attacks (on-screen caption "Bonus burst damage to basic attacks"; narration calls it Heptasis). [04:38] [T]
  - It can be bought before the Hunter-Strike-tier item, or can REPLACE it entirely. [04:46] [T]
- That finishes the core items for physical heroes. [04:54] [T]

### Magic heroes
- DPS mages: need items that give magic damage and cooldown reduction (on screen). [05:02] [T]
  - Split by what the hero needs (on screen "Needs Cooldown" vs "Needs Mana"): [05:10] [T]
    - A lot of cooldown needed: get cooldown boots or magic penetration boots, then the two core items (Enchanted Talisman and Glowing Wand per narration). [05:19] [T]
    - Needs more mana but not a lot of cooldown: buy mana boots, then Glowing Wand (narration). [05:28] [T]
  - Exception (on screen "Exception"): a DPS mage who alternates between basic attacks and skills and so does not rely heavily on mana; he/she needs high-damage items that synergise with the kit. [05:35] [T]
- Control mages: need items that disrupt the enemy (on screen: "Need items that can: Disrupt the enemy"). [05:47] [T]
- Burst mages: need items that deal the highest burst damage possible (on screen: "Deal the highest burst damage possible"). [06:00] [T]
  - Tip #4/5: "Sky Piercer is optional" ; buy it as your second item if and only if no one on your team already has it. [06:10] [T]
- Sustain mages: the build is very similar to physical sustained heroes but instead of the physical sustain axe and the wings item you buy the "concentrated energy" item. [06:22] [T]
- That is how to buy core items for every damage-dealing hero. [06:36] [T]

### Tanks
- Defensive tanks: EVERY item is situational (on screen). [06:48] [T]
  - Check the enemy's main damage source. [06:48] [T]
  - Prioritise the heroes you will face the most. [06:48] [T]
- Counter list ("Counter Tutorial" slide) for defensive tanks, one damage source -> a defence item -> its upgrade: [07:04] [T]
  - vs physical basic-attack critical: leg-plate item, upgrade to armor item (see table). [07:11] [T]
  - vs physical basic-attack attack speed: shield item, upgrade to Dominance Ice (narration). [07:16] [T]
  - vs physical skill burst: armor item, upgrade (narration "dominance ice"; on-screen label "Antique Cuirass"). [07:26] [T]
  - vs physical skill sustain: shield item, upgrade to Dominance Ice (narration). [07:30] [T]
  - vs DPS mages / control mages: silence-robe item, upgrade to Radiant Armor. [07:34] [T]
  - vs burst mages: silence-robe item, upgrade to Athena's Shield. [07:46] [T]
  - vs sustain mages: no counter item - "you'll just have to play". [07:56] [T]
- Offensive tanks: two options; the rest of the build is situational (on screen: bracket "Situational" over the remaining slots). [08:02] [T]
  - If you fight often: thunder belt-type item (on screen label "Fight often:", boots + belt icon). [08:10] [T]
  - If you like staying close to enemies: cursed-helmet-type item (on screen label "Staying close to enemies:", boots + helmet icon). [08:14] [T]
- Once you can tell which damage source needs which item, the remaining question is how to read a hero's damage source: use scaling (next section). [08:21] [T]

### Scaling (Tip #5/5: how to tell which hero has which damage source)
- Scaling = how much a hero's power increases based on a specific stat. [08:30] [T]
- Method: check your skills and see what stat they scale with. [08:56] [T]
- Examples (on screen): [08:38] [T]
  - Julian's passive deals more damage the higher his physical attack, so it scales with physical attack.
  - Lolita's shield gets bigger the higher her magic power, so it scales with magic power.
- This is the secret behind weird metas: tank Lancelot, magic-damage Bane, sustain Julian. [09:03] [T]

## Terms & definitions
- Core items: items you should buy in every single game no matter what your team or the enemy is doing. [00:45]
- Situational items: items bought based on specific conditions. [00:45]
- Basic attack hero: a hero whose skills boost his/her basic attacks. [01:15]
- On-hit item: an item that gives an extra effect every time your basic attack hits (examples on screen: slows enemies; deals bonus damage based on the enemy's HP; triples all on-hit effects). [02:23]
- Scaling: how much a hero's power increases based on a specific stat. [08:30]
- Damage source: node of the on-screen tree classifying how a hero deals damage. [06:48]
- Purely Skills (burst) vs Weave In Basic Attack (burst): whether the burst hero uses skills only or mixes in basic attacks. [04:06]
- Counter Tutorial: on-screen title for the damage-source -> counter item list. [07:04]

## Worked examples

Weaving hero: same plus the "bonus burst damage to basic attacks" item before or instead of Hunter Strike. [04:14] [T]
- DPS mage that needs a lot of cooldown: cooldown boots or magic-penetration boots first; one that needs mana but little cooldown: mana boots first. [05:19] [T]
