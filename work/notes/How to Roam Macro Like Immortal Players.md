# How to Roam Macro Like Immortal Players
Source: How to Roam Macro Like Immortal Players.mp4 | ~9:30 | Roam macro by roamer type (Tank / Heal / Damage) across draft, early game (first turtle, after-turtle rotation, second turtle), mid game (teamfight task) and late game (lord).

## Summary
- The video splits roamers into three types (Tank, Heal, Damage). Every macro decision (rotation path, what to do at turtle, teamfight task, late-game job) depends on which type you play.
- Draft: pick Tank Roamer if team has fewer than 2 tank heroes; otherwise pick Heal or Damage Roamer (prefer whichever the meta favours). Roam item follows roamer type and team strategy.
- Early game: each type has a different first-clear path, a different job at the first turtle, a rotation after the turtle, and a different job at the second turtle.
- Mid game: Tank = punish enemy damage-dealer mistakes; Heal = stay with your damage dealer / mid laner; Damage = flank and pick off enemy damage dealer.
- Late game rotates around the lord: Tank in jungle near lord, Heal behind the marksman, Damage clears far lane / split pushes, all targeting the enemy marksman.
- Video structure (on-screen timeline "Roam Macro"): Draft -> Early Game -> Mid Game -> Late Game. ### Early game macro: before the first turtle

#### Tank Roamer
- [02:05] [T] Start: help your mid laner clear the first wave.
- [02:10] [T] Then split by hero quality:
  - Hero is "Mobile & Strong" in the early game: invade the enemy jungler first, then rotate to the gold lane and deal damage to the gold laner for a bit, then rotate to mid and the exp lane.
  - Hero is "Weak" early: rotate straight to the exp lane (no invade, no gold-lane stop).
- [02:35] [T] Regardless of hero: your focus is to control the river; open all the bushes and light up the map; if an enemy passes through, attack and block their rotation.
- [02:35] [T] Contrast: a tank roamer opens the map; a heal roamer should not (see below).

#### Heal Roamer
- [02:45] [T] Do not open the map or push forward too much: your early game is fragile (on-screen: "Shouldn't open the map or push forward too much").
- [02:55] [T] Start: help clear the first mid wave, then decide by whether your hero has damage:
  - Hero with "decent damage" in the early game: rotate to the exp lane so it is easier to win the turtle.
  - Hero with "no damage": rotate to the gold lane, because you would be useless in the exp lane and might even feed the enemy exp laner.
- [03:22] [T] Exception stated in the video: for one specific hero (transcript: "Diggy"; name in table) the heal roamer does NOT help the mid laner at the start; goes straight to the gold lane and sticks to the enemy gold laner to starve the enemy gold laner of gold ("make the enemy gold laner broke"); keep doing this until the turtle spawns. (Hero name unconfirmed, see Uncertain.)

#### Damage Roamer
- [03:39] [T] Different from tank/heal: always open the map toward the enemy buff in the early game.
- [03:39] [T] If the enemy roamer does not help the enemy jungler: 1v1 the enemy jungler to try to steal their buff.
- [03:45] [T] If the enemy roamer does help (the jungler): just open the map as much as possible, then rotate to the gold lane.
- [03:57] [T] Because you deal a ton of damage you can finish off the enemy gold laner right away on arrival.
- [04:05] [T] TIP 2/3: Never stay in one lane. Damage roamers always have skills with crazy burst; each time you rotate to a lane, unload your burst skills on the enemy, then escape and move to another lane to do the same.

### First turtle ("How to First Turtle")
- [04:25] [T] Your focus changes when the first turtle spawns.
- [04:33] [T] Tank Roamer: open all the bushes around the turtle. If the enemy jungler is there, go all in; use all your skills to zone the enemy jungler, do not let them through.
- [04:45] [T] Heal Roamer: your focus is NOT to zone the enemy jungler but to keep your own jungler alive as long as possible; focus all your heals on them.
- [04:57] [T] Damage Roamer: focus on finishing off the enemy jungler. Be careful not to open the map like a tank roamer; just be ready to go all in if the enemy jungler shows up.

### Second turtle ("How to Second Turtle")
- [05:30] [T] The second turtle spawns a fixed time after the first (number in table); pay close attention.
- [05:38] [T] Tank or Damage Roamer: focus on finishing off the enemy jungler (on-screen: "Focus on finishing off the enemy jungler").
  - [05:46] [T] Common mistake ("Roamer Mistake" on-screen over turtle footage): hesitating while waiting for the right moment. That mindset gets your own jungler picked off by the enemy first.
  - [05:46] [T] Do not be stingy with spells; use everything you have to finish off the enemy jungler.
- [05:59] [T] Heal Roamer: your focus is not the jungler anymore but your EXP laner (on-screen: "Your focus isn't on the jungler anymore, but on your EXP laner"). Use all your heal and utility on them so they can zone safely.
- [06:08] [T] Do the same rotation and turtle strategy until the early game ends.

### Mid game macro
- [06:17] [T] In the mid game your team's rotation has changed, so you must adjust your rotation.
- [06:24] [T] Tank Roamer: you are super tanky because the enemy does not have penetration yet, so do not be afraid to "make a mess" and open the map.
- [06:32] [T] Heal Roamer: stick with your mid laner. You and your mid laner are easy to pick off; sticking together lets you protect each other. Never go alone, because in the mid game the enemy jungler is at their strongest.
  - [06:45] [T] TIP 3/3: Only open the map if you have the right skills (the video shows heroes whose first skill makes this safe; hero list in table).
- [06:59] [T] Damage Roamer: the mid game is when you shine, because the enemy mage and marksman do not have defense items yet while you already have two items, so do not hesitate to fight.

### Teamfight tasks ("How to Teamfight", mid game)
- [07:15] [T] Your task in a fight depends on your hero type.
  | Roamer | Task |
  |---|---|
  | Tank | Look for mistakes from the enemy damage dealers (standing in front of the tank, walking into bushes, not keeping their distance); go all in when they are out of position |
  | Heal | Protect your damage dealer: always stand near them and focus on protecting them |
  | Damage | Flank the enemy damage dealer and pick them off, so the chance of winning the war is higher |
- [07:48] [T] Learn this, because it carries into the late game where rotation is built around the lord.

### Late game macro (rotation around the lord)
- [07:55] [T] Tank Roamer:
  - Always be in the jungle area close to the lord.
  - Look at your marksman: if your marksman is flanking, your task is to follow and flank with them; if not, focus on opening the map in the lord area.
  - Do not worry about the far lane. Do not open a fight alone; make sure the team is ready to follow up before starting a war.
  - Target: the enemy marksman. Do not be stingy with skills, do not wait for the perfect moment; go all in on the enemy marksman.
- [08:36] [T] Heal Roamer:
  - Always stay in the back; let the tank heroes open the map and the mobile heroes clear the far lane.
  - Your task: keep your marksman alive as long as possible; stick to them wherever they are.
- [08:51] [T] Damage Roamer:
  - You can clear the far lane. If your hero can push lane well (examples in table), split push to bait the enemy back before rotating straight to the lord.
  - Be careful: the enemy already has defense items, so you cannot burst them like in the early and mid game.
- [09:14] [T] On-screen at the end: "Completed" checklist: Immortal Macro, Gold Lane Macro, Exp Lane Macro, Mid Lane Macro, Jungle Macro, Roam Macro (part of a series; this video completes Roam Macro). Channel plugs membership for early access to future videos.

## Terms & definitions
- Tank Roamer [00:30]: roamer that absorbs a lot of damage; goes full tank, no damage items.
- Heal Roamer [00:50]: roamer that heals or shields allies.
- Damage Roamer [01:05]: roamer that deals a ton of damage; burst-skill based.
- "Open the map" / "light up the map" [02:35]: clear bushes (vision) so enemy movement is visible; tank roamer's early job, heal roamer should avoid it early.
- "Mobile & Strong" vs "Weak" (tank roamer early game) [02:10]: on-screen labels for tank heroes that can/cannot invade early.
- "Decent Damage" vs "No Damage" (heal roamer early game) [03:05]: on-screen labels deciding exp lane vs gold lane.
- Pick-off / Dive strategy vs Poke / F2B (front-to-back) [01:50]: team-strategy labels used for tank/damage roam item choice (circle = Dive, X = Poke, square = F2B in on-screen icons).
- Far lane [08:14]: the lane away from the lord (late game).
- Split push [09:07]: pushing a lane alone to bait the enemy back before rotating to the lord.
