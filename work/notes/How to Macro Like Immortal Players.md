# How to Macro Like Immortal Players
Source: How to Macro Like Immortal Players.mp4 | ~10:52 | Basic macro every role must know: map awareness per role, strong/weak side, rotation types, turret values, action priority, lane/turret pushing methods, teamfight strategies, and minimizing losses (video is "tips" #1-#8 with minimap diagrams and Esports World Cup / MPL clips).

## Summary
- Macro = knowledge that affects the whole team: rotation direction, objectives, lane-push method, teamfight strategy, turret-pushing tactics.
- Play safe on the weak side, aggressive on the strong side (where your jungler, mid and roamer are); strong side decides rotation paths.
- Rotate to help a winning lane by default; turret values differ (mid outer/inner most valuable, mid inhibitor least); priority order of actions: guaranteed kill > plating > minions/crab > likely kill > turret.
- Three teamfight strategies form a counter triangle: grouping counters pickoff, split push counters grouping, pickoff counters split push.
- Turret pushing: 3-lane push before the 18th minute, straight push (escort the Lord as 5) once the Lord is evolved; sometimes macro means conceding an objective to minimize losses.

### Strong side vs weak side [02:02-02:33]
- Map is divided into two areas: mid-to-gold lane and mid-to-XP lane. [02:09] [T]
- The area where your jungler, mid and roamer are located = strong side; the opposite = weak side. [02:09] [T]
- Weak side: play safe. Strong side: play more aggressively because teammates are nearby to help. [02:18] [T]
- Strong side is crucial because it determines rotation paths. [02:26] [T]
- Pro players can give you different rotation directions, but the basics fall into three types. [02:33] [T]

### Three rotation types [02:41-03:48]
1. Help a losing lane [02:49] [T]
   - Assist the lane that is behind to stabilize it and stop it falling further. [02:49] [T]
   - Caution: only works if you know the teammate is skilled enough to bounce back once levels are equal. [02:56] [T]
2. Help a winning lane (preferred over 1) [03:03] [T]
   - Lets teammates snowball harder; help them secure everything: crabs, plating, kills. [03:10] [T]
   - Let the losing lane play safe even if they fall behind; the other four members of the team can carry. [03:10] [T]
3. Rotate based on the Turtle (the fallback when unsure) [03:25] [T]
   - If the Turtle is on the XP lane, help the XP laner, and vice versa. [03:25] [T]
   - The pressured lane lets your laner assist with the Turtle. [03:34] [T]
- Tip #4/8: no matter where your rotation goes, communicate. [03:41] [T]
  - e.g. tell the team Franco has used his ultimate so the assassin can engage. [03:41] [T]
  - e.g. tell the team the enemy lacks Flicker so the team can dive. [03:48] [T]
- Every rotation has a purpose: either helping a teammate or securing an objective. [03:55] [T]

### Turret values [03:55-05:22]
- Question: destroy the mid turret or a side turret? They look the same but turrets have different values; names are Outer, Inner, Inhibitor. [04:03] [T]
- Outer and Inner mid lane turrets are the most valuable: they open up the map between the lanes and the buff area. [04:10] [T]
- Tip #5/8, consequences if Outer and Inner mid turrets are gone: [04:25] [T]
  - Your mid and roamer have a hard time rotating. [04:33] [T]
  - Your marksmen and XP laners become easy targets. [04:33] [T]
  - Your jungler struggles to farm and the enemy jungler can steal your jungle. [04:40] [T]
- The mid inhibitor turret is the least valuable. [04:47] [T]
  - Super minions on gold or XP lane after the inhibitor falls: the enemy is forced to monitor that lane continuously, making it easier for you to secure the Lord. [04:54] [T]
  - Super minions in the mid lane: you have a hard time gathering minions to push because mid lane is shorter; the enemy benefits as the pushed super minions give them a lot of gold. [05:00] [T]
  - Video diagram (05:05): side-lane super minions: the enemy hero at the side lane is labelled "Far Away" from the Lord pit (so you can secure the Lord); mid-lane super minions march along the short mid path toward the enemy. [05:05] [T]
- Answer: mid for Outer turret, mid for Inner turret, side lanes for Inhibitor. [05:22] [T]

### Action priority (what to do first) [05:22-06:14]
- Five options on a side lane: take the plating, destroy the target turret, farm minions, duel enemy heroes, secure the crab. [05:22] [T]
- Priority list shown on screen (the video's order): [05:35] [T]
  1. Enemy heroes (kill 100% guaranteed). [05:35] [T]
  2. Plating (reason: it disappears after a time limit; see table). [05:35] [T]
  3. Minions and crab (gold and experience). [05:35] [T]
  4. Enemy heroes (when the kill chance is high). [05:35] [T]
  5. Turrets (they last; they will still be there). [05:35] [T]
- Exception: if you are in the mid lane, mid turrets are more valuable than minions because of the map control they provide. [05:55] [T]
- That is why MPL players love standing in the bushes: better to secure guaranteed gold than risk a duel you might not win. [06:05] [T]

### Turret vs crab: gold math [06:14-07:20]
- Pushing the turret is not always the best option; do the math. [06:14] [T]
- Option A, destroy the turret: gold from kill + gold from minions + gold from turret, minus the gold the respawning enemy gets from the pushed wave (because the turret is gone the wave survives for them). Net difference shown: 370 (200+200+110 = 510, minus 140 enemy wave gold). [06:21] [T]
- Option B, take the crab: gold from the crab + gold from minions, the enemy gets no gold because the minions die to the turret. Net difference: 400 on XP lane, 460 on gold lane (gold lane crab). [06:40] [T]
- Conclusion for this example: the crab is better than the turret. [06:55] [T]
- Condition noted: in this example the plating is already gone and the minions are still carrying the cannon. [06:55] [T]
  - If plating is still available, prioritize taking it. [07:05] [T]
  - If the minions no longer carry the wagon (Tip #6/8: "if the minions no longer carry the wagon"), it is better to destroy the turret. [07:05] [T]

### Lane pushing methods [07:12-07:42]
- Hard push: push the minions as fast as possible; the push-the-turret technique above is a hard push. [07:12] [T]
- Slow push: minions gather and slowly push once there are enough of them; great in a lane that is far from objectives. [07:19] [T]
- Hard push and slow push are lane pushing methods, not turret pushing methods; understand both because they are used when learning how to push turrets (Tip #7/8). [07:27] [T]

### Teamfight strategies [07:42-08:50]
- Decisions are wasted if you do not understand how to fight properly; three types. [07:42] [T]
- Pickoff strategy: isolate and eliminate an enemy hero who is alone; usually a 3v1 gank; the other two teammates farm and defend the base. [07:57] [T]
  - Works well when your team is behind. [08:04] [T]
- Grouping strategy: the entire team gathers for a 5v5 fight; counters pickoff. [08:12] [T]
  - Works well when your team is ahead because the chance of winning the fight is high. [08:18] [T]
- Split push strategy: some teammates push turrets alone, constantly pressuring a single lane; counters grouping. [08:26] [T]
  - Works well when your team is behind and wants to avoid teamfights. [08:33] [T]
  - Make sure your hero can escape (examples given: Sun/Ling and Aulus). [08:40] [T]
- Counter triangle shown on screen: Grouping counters Pickoff; Split push counters Grouping; Pickoff counters Split push. [08:15-08:30] [T]
- All strategies serve one goal: destroy the enemy base. [08:48] [T]

### Turret pushing methods [08:55-09:45]
- Most players push turrets carelessly making the push easy to defend; understand turret pushing methods. [08:55] [T]
- Screen shows "Before 18th Minute vs After 18th Minute". [09:05] [T]
- Before the 18th minute: 3 Lane Push. [09:05] [T]
  - Goal: push all lanes at the same time, confusing the enemy about which lane to defend. [09:09] [T]
  - Sequence: when the Lord spawns: hard push gold lane, hard push XP lane, slow push mid lane. [09:09] [T]
  - Once the side lane minions reach the marked point (on the minimap diagram, around the enemy side), hard push the mid lane so all three waves arrive together. [09:17] [T]
  - Diagram (09:20): two heroes on the XP lane (top-left) and two on the gold lane (bottom-right) with the remaining heroes in mid. [09:20] [T]
- After the 18th minute / once the Lord evolves: switch to Straight Push. [09:24] [T]
  - Escort the Lord as a team of five (diagram 09:34: all five heroes together around base lane). [09:24] [T]
  - Reason: an evolved Lord gains damage reduction when teammates are nearby. [09:32] [T]

### Minimizing losses (Tip #8/8) [09:45-10:34]
- Everything so far is macro to gain advantages; in real games it is not always smooth; sometimes macro is used to minimize losses. [09:45] [T]
- Example: Team Liquid Philippines let the Turtle go to ONIC Indonesia in the Esports World Cup. [09:53] [T]
  - Why: the Turtle fight did not make sense; JP died alone making the Turtle fight 4v5. [10:00] [T]
  - Rather than losing the Turtle fight and letting ONIC make a comeback, Liquid let it go to avoid further losses; they kept their advantage and won the game. [10:08] [T]
- Applies to solo players too: if your team suddenly fights alone while your jungler is not ready, or if a jungler forces/tries to steal the Turtle, it is better to leave them and avoid further losses. [10:20] [T]

## Terms & definitions
- Strong side: area (mid-to-gold or mid-to-XP) where your jungler, mid and roamer are; play aggressively there. [02:09] [T]
- Weak side: the opposite area; play safe. [02:18] [T]
- Outer / Inner / Inhibitor turret: turret tiers in Land of Dawn; shown on the minimap labelled "Outer" (closer to your side) and "Inner". [04:03] [T]
- Hard push: push the minions as fast as possible. [07:12] [T]
- Slow push: let minions gather and push slowly once there are enough. [07:19] [T]
- Pickoff / Grouping / Split push: the three teamfight strategies above. [07:57] [T]
- 3 Lane Push: push all three lanes simultaneously; Straight Push: five heroes escort the Lord to push one path. [09:09] [T]
- Super minions: spawn on the lane whose inhibitor turret is destroyed. [04:47] [T]
- Plating: turret armor layer; taking it is worth gold. [05:35] [T]
