# Every MLBB Term Explained
Source: Every MLBB Term Explained.mp4 | ~12:25 | Glossary video: MLBB terms in order of a match (draft -> early -> mid -> late game), mostly "X vs Y" definition cards plus short clips. Contains almost no strategy beyond definitions.

## Summary
- Vocabulary video: ~13 "Versus" comparison cards (burst/DPS, heal/anti-heal, immune/untargetable, on-hit/critical, damage reduction/physical attack reduction, blind/draft ban, lifesteal/spell vamp/hybrid lifesteal, Lord minions/super minions, etc.) plus single-term cards.
- Structure: Draft terms (bans, picks, win rate, trolls) -> Early game (micro/macro, wave control, farming, ganking, buffs, power spike, snowball, bounty) -> Mid game (clashes, flank, split/straight/3-lane push) -> Late game (Lord minions vs super minions).
- Phases: early game -> mid game (until the Lord spawns) -> late game (after the Lord spawns); exact minutes in the LIVE table.
- Video gives the three wave-control states (slow push / hard push / freeze) with a minion-count rule, and the KDA and win-rate definitions.
- Opening card groups terms into "Popular Terms" (Jungler, Hero, Heal, Exp, Burst, Gold, Minion, Mage, MM) and "Epic Player Terms" (Core, Anti Heal, TB ...); later tiers are mentioned (immortal-level terms) but not all are shown.

## Tactical knowledge

### Hero / damage concepts
- Burst damage = damage that comes out as much as possible, as fast as possible. [00:00] [T]
- DPS (damage per second) = damage that comes out bit by bit over time (opposite of burst). [00:15] [T]
- Meta = "most effective tactic available". Meta heroes are very strong; off-meta heroes are considered very weak. [00:35] [T]
  - Rule: in any meta, very strong heroes must be picked or banned -> "pick or ban heroes". On-screen: "Very strong Heroes must be Picked or Banned." [01:05] [T]
- Hero roles/strategies: [01:05] [T]
  - Jungler = a role that uses Retribution and farms in the jungle.
  - Core = your team's main damage dealer.
  - Hyper = a strategy where every resource is given to one person so they can carry the team.
- Hero types (card "Hero Type" lists 4; shown: Poke, then three hidden "???" that are explained as sustained, tanky, initiator): [01:26] [T]
  - Poke = good at dealing small amounts of damage to the enemy.
  - Sustained = good at surviving thanks to healing or shields.
  - Tanky = high health or defense. "Sustained heroes survive using heal and shield; tanky heroes survive with health and defense."
  - Initiator = hero that starts fights with their skills (examples named: Belerick, Atlas, Tigreal "with their ultimates"). Rule: "Usually, tanky heroes are also initiators." [02:06] [T]
- Heal = restoring your health bar. Anti-heal = reduces the enemy's healing. Shield = temporary protection that absorbs damage. [01:44] [T]
- Crowd control = effect that hinders the enemy's movement; it does not affect heroes who are immune. [02:07] [T]
- Immune vs untargetable (Versus #5/13): [02:28] [T]
  - Immune = unaffected by things like damage or crowd control; you can still be attacked, the attack is just useless. Good for tanks (you still absorb/soak their attention and damage).
  - Untargetable = enemies cannot attack/target you, so you cannot absorb their damage. Good for squishy heroes like assassins and marksmen.
- On-hit vs critical (Versus #6/13): [02:54] [T]
  - On-hit = effect that enhances your basic attacks (e.g. a passive that adds extra damage to every basic attack). On-hit heroes usually rely on attack speed.
  - Critical = damage boost to your basic attacks that occurs based on chance.
  - Critical damage = bonus multiplier; 100% critical damage means damage is doubled.
  - Critical chance = likelihood the hit is critical; 50% crit chance = 1 out of every 2 attacks crits. On-screen example: Critical Chance 50% -> sequence 100, 200, 100, 200, 100, 200 (non-crit 100, crit 200). [03:08] [T]
  - Tip #1/8: "Criticals are useless if you don't scale correctly." [03:35] [T]
- Scale = how much your hero gets stronger based on a particular stat. Example: higher physical attack -> more damage for a hero that scales with physical attack (Granger named). On-screen illustration: +100 Physical Attack deals 170 Physical Damage; +200 Physical Attack deals 220 Physical Damage (illustrates that damage depends on the scaling stat). [03:41] [T]
- Damage types: [03:53] [T]
  - Physical attack -> physical damage (shown in red).
  - Magic power -> magic damage (shown in blue).
  - Adaptive damage = deals physical damage if your physical attack is higher than your magic power, and vice versa (magic otherwise).
  - True damage = most powerful; penetrates all defensive stats; shown in white.
- Counters to damage (Versus #7/13): [04:16] [T]
  - Damage reduction = you receive less damage.
  - Physical attack reduction = if the enemy is given physical attack reduction, they deal less physical damage (on-screen illustration: 100 damage vs 80 damage, "Without Physical Attack Reduction" vs "With").

### Draft: bans
- Blind ban = both teams ban at the same time, so you do not know what the enemy will ban. Used in public matches. [04:36] [T]
- Draft ban = bans alternate between teams. Used in professional matches. [04:51] [T]
- Top bans = first 3 bans; bottom bans = last 2 bans. [04:51] [T]
- Respect ban (Tip #2/8) = a hero banned not because it is strong, but because the enemy is exceptionally good at using it (examples given with player-hero pairs; see Uncertain). [05:06] [T]

### Draft: picks
- Draft pick = taking turns picking heroes with the enemy; you can see the enemy's picks and can choose the same heroes. [05:21] [T]
- Flex pick = picking a flexible hero, one that can be played in more than one role or position. [05:21] [T]
- Blind pick = choosing a lane without knowing the enemy matchup. [05:36] [T]
- Counter pick = choosing a specific hero that is very strong against an enemy hero; called "the most important type of pick". [05:36] [T]
- Last pick = being last to pick (the "unlucky" slot). You can request your team to swap pick orders. [05:51] [T]
  - To request a swap you need to show your win rate (the pick screen displays per-hero matches and win rate; on-screen example: 104 matches, 75% win rate). [05:51] [T]
- Win rate = how often you win = Total Win / Total Match x 100%. [06:09] [T]
- Troll = player who prevents you from winning, usually on purpose. Your win rate will be low if you encounter many trolls. [06:09] [T]
- Lose streak = losing multiple games in a row; win streak is the opposite. [06:20] [T]
- Draft rule: a team that drafts four marksmen will be dodged (the game will not happen). [06:20] [T]

### Early game structure
- Early game = the opening phase in the Land of Dawn, while plating still exists and siege minions still give extra gold and exp (durations in LIVE table). [06:48] [T]
- Most important early-game topic: micro mechanics and macro skills. [06:48] [T]
  - Micro mechanics = your knowledge of a particular hero: skills, matchups, power spikes, scalings, items, damage types, hidden mechanics.
  - Macro skills = skills that affect the game as a whole: rotations, farming patterns, objectives, split pushing, teamfights, wave control.
- Plating = the golden layer on the tower; attacking it gives a bit of gold. [07:10] [T]
- Other ways to get gold: farm jungle monsters or lane minions. [07:10] [T]

### Wave control (lane)
- Minions spawn every few seconds in a group; one group = a wave. [07:10] [T]
- Cutting = reaching the minions before they get to the center of the lane. [07:10] [T]
- If the wave reaches the center, you have three options: slow push, hard push, freeze. [07:24] [T]
  - Slow push = saving up minions to build a massive wave; do this by making sure you have just one more minion than the enemy. [07:24] [T]
  - Hard push = clearing the enemy minions as quickly as possible so your minions get shoved to the enemy turret (the video says "enemy third", i.e. enemy tower; see Uncertain). [07:38] [T]
  - Freeze = keep the minions in place by making sure your minion count equals the enemy's. [07:38] [T]
- Tip #3/8: the one thing you must do when farming is deny. [07:54] [T]
  - Denying = making the enemy lose minions and gold, usually by zoning them out.
  - Zoning = keeping the enemy from entering a specific area (the lane, the turtle pit, or the Lord pit). [08:08] [T]
- Last hit = dealing the final blow needed to get the minion (gold). [08:08] [T]
- Lane also involves fighting the enemy's laner: killing them 1v1 = solo kill. [08:22] [T]

### Fighting / survival in lane
- KDA = Kill Death Assist: a ratio that shows how often you kill or assist compared to how often you die. (On-screen: kills and assists in green, deaths in red; formula was shown but not legible in the frames checked; see Uncertain.) [08:22] [T]
- Ganking = going to another lane to help your team kill the enemy; the route you take is called pathing. [08:50] [T]
- To survive enemy ganks, play safe; the best way is lifesteal. [08:50] [T]
- Lifesteal vs spell vamp vs hybrid lifesteal (Versus #10/13): [08:50] [T]
  - Lifesteal = heals you when you deal damage with basic attacks.
  - Spell vamp = heals you when you deal damage with skills.
  - Hybrid lifesteal = strongest; heals you from any kind of damage.
- Dive = entering the enemy turret to secure a kill. Prevention: your roamer should peel for you. [09:18] [T]
- Peel = guarding your teammate from enemy attacks. [09:18] [T]

### Jungler, tempo and snowball
- If you play jungler, know three buffs (effects are [L]; see table): blue buff, red buff, turtle buff. With them your team reaches its power spike faster. [09:18] [T]
- Power spike = when your hero is at their strongest; can happen in early, mid or late game. [09:46] [T]
- To capitalize on a power spike you need to invade = reading the enemy jungle to steal their camps. [09:46] [T]
- Snowball = winning from the early game and using that advantage to grow it even further. [09:46] [T]
- Caution when snowballing: your bounty will be very high. Bounty = the extra gold the enemy gets when they kill someone who is way ahead; a huge bounty lets them make a comeback. [10:08] [T]
- Comeback = turning a losing situation into a winning one. [10:08] [T]
- Stacks = accumulated effects/bonuses that increase with time or actions (example clip: Aldous' first skill gives 1 stack when used to last hit; stacks make him stronger). [10:25] [T]

### Mid game
- Mid game = from the end of early game until the Lord spawns (minute numbers in LIVE table). [10:43] [T]
- Clashes/wars = fights involving more than 2 people; some players on the front line, some on the back line. [10:43] [T]
- Best thing you can do in a clash: lock the enemy back line = focus on one specific enemy. [11:01] [T]
- Flank = attacking from the side or back when the enemy is not ready (different from front-to-front clash). Enemy can still kite = attack while moving. [11:01] [T]
- Rule: if you want your flank to succeed, use conceal (an effect that makes your hero invisible). [11:01] [T]
- Conceal can also be used to pick off the enemy = take them out one by one. [11:01] [T]
- Split push = pushing the farthest lane to pressure the map. Warning: be careful attacking because the enemy can split push. [11:30] [T]
- Straight push = pushing one lane with the whole team. [11:30] [T]
- 3-lane push = splitting up to push all lanes; only viable if you have the Lord. [11:30] [T]

### Late game
- Late game starts after the Lord spawns; the Lord later evolves (timing in LIVE table). [11:58] [T]
- Lord minions = enhanced minions after defeating the Lord. Super minions = appear when you destroy the enemy's inhibitor. Lord minions are stronger than super minions. [12:08] [T]

## Worked examples
- Respecting a pick in draft: ban a hero not because it is strong, but because the enemy player is exceptionally good with it (video cites specific player-hero pairs). -> reason: that pairing is disproportionately dangerous. [05:06] [T]
- Last-pick situation: you drew the last pick -> request a pick-order swap -> show your win rate (pick screen displays hero match count and win rate) to justify. [05:51] [T]
- Wave states: wave arrives at the center -> keep one more minion than the enemy to slow push/build a massive wave; match the enemy's count to freeze; clear enemy minions fast to hard push. [07:24] [T]
- Safe laning vs gank: expect gank -> play safe with lifesteal; expect dive -> roamer peels. [08:50] [T]
- Flank with conceal: flank succeeds if you can approach invisibly; conceal also enables picking off enemies one by one. [11:01] [T]
- 3-lane push is only valid when your team has the Lord. [11:30] [T]
- Crit scaling: a 50% crit chance hero does 100, 200, 100, 200... on screen; but crit is useless without scaling stat. [03:08] [T]
