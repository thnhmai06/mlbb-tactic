# I Studied Every Setting So You Don't Have To
Source: I Studied Every Setting So You Don't Have To.mp4 | ~7:32 | A walkthrough of MLBB's combat/targeting settings (what each one does, what to choose, why), ending with a recommended combination and a short "leave it to personal preference" list.

## Summary
- Wrong settings, not bad play, cause many "misplays" (ulting the wrong target, hitting a tank with a single-target skill, a marksman running back and forth, Fanny feeling slow).
- Core recommendation set: Advanced targeting method, a Targeting Priority matched to your hero type, Hero Lock ON, Accurate Indicator ON, Aim Panning OFF, Assisted Aiming Mode OFF, Close Pursuit OFF, Auto Skill Upgrade OFF, Auto Buy OFF, HP Shift ON, HP Lock ON.
- For basic attacks: Basic Attack Smart Targeting = Lock-on Targeting, Attack Activation Distance = Distant. The video's "Settings Combo" is Advanced Mode + Moving Pursuit + Basic Attack Smart Targeting (see Tactical knowledge).
- Personal-preference settings (no recommendation): Joystick Dead Zone (called "not really important"), Skill Joystick Position, Fixed joystick position, Skill Sensitivity, Camera Sensitivity. Active Creeps Filter is called "not very useful".
- The video shows the settings as stylised overlays and never shows the in-game menu path (no menu navigation shown). The names/labels below are as written in the overlays.

## Tactical knowledge

### Targeting Method (Free / Advanced / Lock)
- [00:28] [T] The targeting method has 3 modes; extra buttons beyond the one basic attack button depend on the mode.
  - [00:36] [T] Free Mode: only 1 attack button.
  - [00:40] [T] Advanced Mode: 2 extra buttons. A tower button (hits turrets only) and a minion button (hits minions).
    - [00:50] [T] Caution: the tower button cannot attack enemy heroes. The minion button will attack heroes if no minions are nearby.
  - [01:04] [T] Lock Mode: a hero button that switches the selected enemy hero, and a minion button that switches the selected minion.
- [01:10] [T] Recommendation: Advanced Mode. The video says that after watching dozens of pro-player live streams, all of them use Advanced Mode "because it's way better when combined with another setting" (the combo below).

### Targeting Priority (what basic attacks / auto-target pick) — choose by hero type
- [01:30] [T] Priority is not one-size-fits-all; the best choice depends on hero type.
- Definitions of the three options:
  - [01:30] [T] Lowest HP Percentage: targets the enemy with the lowest HP percentage.
  - [01:44] [T] Lowest HP: targets the enemy with the least absolute HP.
  - [01:52] [T] Nearest Target: always attacks the closest enemy; HP is irrelevant.
- Recommended choice per hero type (as shown on the icon row, 02:00-02:12):
  - [01:52] [T] Marksman: Nearest Target (to keep your distance from the enemy).
  - [01:52] [T] DPS Mage: Nearest Target (DPS) (same reason, keep distance).
  - [02:04] [T] Burst Mage: Lowest HP (Burst) (to finish off low-HP enemies).
  - [02:04] [T] Assassin: Lowest HP.
  - [02:04] [T] Fighter: Lowest HP.
  - [02:11] [T] Tank / Support: free to choose either mode (no strong recommendation).
- [02:15] [T] All of these targeting priorities are overridden when Hero Lock is used (see next).

### Hero Lock
- [02:18] [T] Hero Lock ON. Once you press the hero button, all your attacks lock onto that hero.
- [02:25] [T] Reason / example: fighting a hero that makes clones (the video uses Sun). With Hero Lock on, your attacks always target the real one.
- [02:32] [T] The video's verdict: "that's why this setting is a must."

### Accurate Indicator
- [02:39] [T] Accurate Indicator ON. When enabled, single-target skills can be aimed more precisely.
- [02:39] [T] Reason: the video blames wrong-target single-target skills on this setting, not on the player (ults the tank instead of the mage; hooks/claws/kicks a tank instead of the intended target). Also called "a must".

### Aim Panning (transcript "Elim Panning") and Assisted Aiming Mode
- [02:54] [T] Aim Panning is different from Accurate Indicator and "can actually mess you up". When enabled, the camera shifts whenever you use a long-range skill.
  - [03:01] [T] It can help with heroes that fire long-range skills occasionally (video names Franco, Layla, Ovaria), but the camera shakes if you spam skills.
  - [03:08] [T] Cost: spamming skills (e.g. Fanny's second skill, Granger's ultimate) makes the camera shake. Fanny players become slow and Granger players struggle to kite.
  - [03:22] [T] Granger's ultimate: tapping it fires bullets based on your analog (joystick) direction.
  - [06:46] [T] Summary screen shows Aim Panning OFF.
- [03:22] [T] Assisted Aiming Mode: if enabled, skills auto-aim. The video says the auto-aim is terrible, so turn it OFF.

### Attack range vs Chase range, Moving Pursuit, Close Pursuit
- [03:37] [T] Every hero has an attack range, and also a chase range (outer ring on the diagram, 03:50).
- [03:45] [T] Rule: if a higher-priority target is outside your attack range but still inside your chase range, your hero ignores nearby targets and chases that one instead.
- Moving Pursuit:
  - [03:53] [T] Makes you chase enemies even while retreating. This is why a marksman keeps running back and forth (the video's explanation of the marksman "moving aimlessly" mentioned at the start).
  - [04:01] [T] "Still, this setting can be really strong when combined with another setting" (the Settings Combo).
- Close Pursuit:
  - [04:14] [T] Overrides/ignores all the targeting-priority methods.
  - [04:20] [T] Example: normally you would attack Miya; with Close Pursuit ON you attack the monster instead because it is closer.
  - [04:27] [T] Risk: can make you hit the wrong target in a teamfight. Recommendation: turn it OFF.

### Joystick Dead Zone
- [04:35] [T] With the setting disabled, the hero keeps moving until you release the joystick. With it ON, you can stop moving by sliding the joystick back to the centre.
- [04:46] [T] The video says this setting "isn't really important" (no recommendation; demo shows it ON).

### Active Creeps Filter
- [04:51] [T] Default basic-attack priority when an enemy hero, a jungle monster and a minion are at the same distance: enemy heroes first, then jungle monsters, minions last.
- [05:06] [T] With Active Creeps Filter ON, the priority becomes: enemy heroes, then jungle monsters that are attacking, then idle jungle monsters, then minions.
- [05:21] [T] Verdict: "isn't very useful".

### Skill Joystick Position
- [05:21] [T] If you tap your skill button slightly off-centre the skill still triggers, but the joystick appears in the middle of the skill area, not where you tap, when set to Fixed.
- [05:36] [T] If set to Tap (tap position), the joystick appears exactly where you tap the skill.
- [05:44] [T] "This one's up to you" (personal preference).

### Basic Attack Smart Targeting + Attack Activation Distance (the combo's third part)
- [05:44] [T] Advanced targeting method and Moving Pursuit "work really well when combined with" Basic Attack Smart Targeting.
- [05:51] [T] Three options:
  - Lock-on Targeting: lets you pick a basic-attack target; once selected, every basic attack stays locked on that target.
  - Manual Targeting: does not lock; you must aim every single basic attack yourself.
  - Off: the feature can be turned off.
- [06:14] [T] Best option: Lock-on Targeting ("lock targeting"), especially for heroes that rely on locking with basic attacks (named: Roger, Hanzo, and every marksman; the on-screen Tips 2/2 shows two hero portraits plus the marksman icon).
- [06:24] [T] Attack Activation Distance adjusts the range of Basic Attack Smart Targeting. Three options:
  - Fixed Position Based on Range: locks onto enemies within basic-attack range.
  - Close: does the same, but also chases targets slightly outside the range.
  - Distant: targets as far as possible.
  - [06:44] [T] Best choice: Distant (the summary screen shows Distant ticked).

### Settings Combo (as titled on screen)
- [01:12] [T] The video teases a "Settings Combo" of three settings with "? + ? + ?" at 01:12, revealed progressively at 04:00 (Advanced Mode first) and 05:50 (Moving Pursuit second).
- [05:44]-[06:14] [T] The three parts, assembled from the narration: Advanced targeting method (Advanced Mode) + Moving Pursuit ON + Basic Attack Smart Targeting (Lock-on Targeting). The third item is revealed on screen at 05:58 as Basic Attack Smart Targeting.
- [04:08] [T] The note: Moving Pursuit alone makes marksmen run back and forth; it is described as strong only in combination.

### Settings that must be turned OFF / ON ("all of the settings we've covered will be useless if you mess up these")
- [06:50] [T] Auto Skill Upgrade: "will level up your skills automatically". Must be OFF so you don't end up with wrong skill levels.
- [06:50] [T] Auto Buy: "will auto buy items when you have enough gold". Must be OFF so you don't end up with the wrong items.
- [07:03] [T] Fixed joystick position: keeps the joystick from moving around. "This one's up to you."
- [07:10] [T] HP Shift: shows the HP of objectives. ON.
- [07:10] [T] HP Lock: shows the HP of heroes. ON.
  - [07:17] [T] Reason (on-screen caption, 07:17): "So you don't miss retributions or hit the wrong target."
- [07:17] [T] Skill Sensitivity and Camera Sensitivity: personal preference (no recommendation; sliders from Low to High shown without values).

## Terms & definitions
- Targeting Method (Free / Advanced / Lock Mode): 1 attack button / 2 extra buttons (tower, minion) / hero + minion switch buttons. [00:28]-[01:17]
- Targeting Priority: the rule used to choose which enemy the attack goes to: Lowest HP Percentage, Lowest HP, Nearest Target. [01:30]
- Hero Lock: pressing the hero button locks all attacks on that hero; overrides targeting priority. [02:18]
- Accurate Indicator: more precise aiming for single-target skills. [02:39]
- Aim Panning: camera shifts when using long-range skills. [02:54]
- Assisted Aiming Mode: skills auto-aim. [03:22]
- Attack range / Chase range: attack range is the hero's range; chase range is a larger ring; targets beyond attack range but inside chase range can pull your hero to chase. [03:37]-[03:53]
- Moving Pursuit: chase enemies even while retreating. [03:53]
- Close Pursuit: attack the closest target, ignoring targeting priority. [04:14]
- Joystick Dead Zone: with it on, sliding the joystick back to centre stops the hero. [04:35]
- Active Creeps Filter: changes basic-attack priority to heroes > attacking jungle monsters > idle jungle monsters > minions. [05:06]
- Skill Joystick Position (Fixed / Tap): where the skill joystick appears. [05:21]
- Basic Attack Smart Targeting (Lock-on / Manual / Off). [05:51]
- Attack Activation Distance (Fixed Position Based on Range / Close / Distant). [06:24]
- Auto Skill Upgrade, Auto Buy. [06:50]
- HP Shift = shows objective HP; HP Lock = shows hero HP. [07:10]
