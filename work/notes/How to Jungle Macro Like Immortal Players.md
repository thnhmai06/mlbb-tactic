# How to Jungle Macro Like Immortal Players
Source: How to Jungle Macro Like Immortal Players.mp4 | ~9:40 | Jungle macro by game phase (draft, early, mid, late): pick by team comp, early routes and invade handling, retribution fights and the turtle, tank vs damage jungler farming/teamfight roles, lord dance, and 3 common mistakes. Built from pro games of KarlTzy (Liquid), Kairi (ONIC) and Yazuke/"Yasuke" (Alter Ego) (on-screen thumbnails, 00:05).

## Summary
- Everything depends on the hero type: tank jungler vs damage jungler (assassin/fighter). Draft the jungler by what the team lacks (tanks) and by comp type (dive / front-to-back / flexible), not by jungler type alone.
- Early game: start from the gold lane side and finish at the XP lane side; win/lose the matchup decides fight vs run; never farm lane minions early; rotate to the XP laner after clearing; the turtle goes to whoever wins the last-hit-damage matchup.
- Before minute 5 all junglers farm the same; after, damage junglers out-farm (jungle + empty-lane minions, alone), tank junglers disrupt (steal enemy monsters, force small fights, do not farm lane minions).
- Retribution fight method: calculate last-hit damage, position well (not inside the pit), CC the enemy jungler right before retribution; ask the team for CC if you have none.
- Teamfight and late game: tank jungler = front line (then secondary tank), do not show on map unless team can back you up; damage jungler = hide, wait, enter last, target marksman > jungler > mage. Late game revolves around the Lord: use the "lord dance" (slow push far lane, stall the lord, poke) to get a retribution advantage instead of a 50/50.

## Tactical knowledge

### Draft
- [00:25] [T] Jungle heroes split into two types: tank junglers and damage-dealing junglers (assassin or fighter). Everything later (routes, teamfights) depends on which you play.
- [00:25] [T] If the team does not yet have two tank heroes, pick a tank jungler. If the team already has enough tanks, pick a damage jungler (assassin or fighter). (On-screen tree "Draft": "If team doesn't have 2 Tank heroes yet: Tank Jungler" / "If team already has enough: Damage Jungler (Assassin / Fighter)", 00:45-00:55.)
- [00:50] [T] Then do not obsess over jungler type: focus on team composition. (On-screen: three rows Dive / Front to Back / "?", 01:10-01:20.)
  - Dive comp -> pick a jungler who can jump onto enemies.
  - Front-to-back comp -> pick a DPS jungler.
  - Unsure of team comp -> pick a jungler that can do both (flexible).
- [01:22] [T] Why it matters: early-game farming routes depend on the hero you play.

### Retribution (battle spell) choice [01:37]
- [01:37] [T] The fight does not start in the jungle, it starts at the first buy: choose red/blue/purple retribution by the hero's needs. On-screen table "Condition -> Retribution" (01:42-01:50):
  - Hero needs extra mobility or needs to kite -> Blue.
  - Hero is agile and wants to 1v1 all the time -> Red.
  - Playing a tank jungler -> Purple. (Said in audio at 01:37-01:53; table third row only partly seen "If you're ...".)

### Early game: first route and invades
- [01:54] [T] Two possible starts (blue side or red side buff). Rule: in the early game always start from the GOLD lane side and go towards the XP lane side.
  - [02:05] [T] If gold lane is on top, use "this route"; if XP lane is on top, use "this route" (animated minimap route drawn from own base through the own jungle, 02:00-02:10; see Uncertain for the exact path).
- [02:17] [T] In-game it will not be smooth because you can get invaded (in-game "Lawan Menginvasi Jungle" = enemy invading jungle banner, 02:15).
- [02:17] [T] If invaded: look at the matchup.
  - Win the matchup -> fight them.
  - Lose the matchup -> just do not die: run away and farm somewhere else (minimap example 02:29: enemy hero comes from top, own jungler retreats down toward own side).
  - The same logic applies when you invade the enemy.
- [02:32] [T] MISTAKE 1/3 (most fatal early mistake): clearing minions in the early game, because the jungler's gold gets cut in half (on-screen "Mistake 1/3" over a jungler hitting lane minions, 02:34-02:38).
- [02:40] [T] After all monsters are cleared, rotate to the XP lane ("Early Game Rotation" minimap: jungler moves next to the XP laner near the enemy-side edge of the turtle area, 02:42-02:50). The XP laner is the one who will carry your early game.
- [02:47] [T] If your XP laner snowballs, you will easily get the turtle: they can zone and pick off enemies.

### Turtle: last-hit damage matchup [02:54]
- [02:54] [T] Key to taking the turtle: the LAST-HIT DAMAGE matchup. Compare yourself with the enemy jungler; every jungler has their own last-hit damage value.
  - [03:08] [T] Last-hit damage = retribution + your hardest-hitting/finishing skill. Examples as the video shows them (on-screen "Last Hit Damage Value", 03:05-03:18): Leomord = first skill + retribution; Hellcurt (transcript spelling) = second skill + retribution; Belmond (transcript spelling) = ultimate + retribution. Numbers in the LIVE table.
- [03:18] [T] Worked example "Leomord vs Baxia" (Round 1/2): Leomord has higher last-hit damage than Baxia, so it is much easier for him to win the retribution fight.
  - If you are the Leomord (higher last-hit): focus the turtle while your roamer and XP laner zone the enemy.
  - If you are the Baxia (lower last-hit): you cannot do that, because if Leomord comes in he easily wins the retribution fight. Better to teamfight first and take the Lord/turtle later.
  - [03:47] [T] Summary rule: if you win in last-hit damage, focus the turtle; if you lose, focus on fighting the enemy.
- [03:54] [T] What if you can neither last-hit nor fight (example "Round 2/2": Ling vs Baxia; on-screen "In the Early Game, Ling can't beat Baxia" in last-hit and teamfight)? Then "crush the map": go to the enemy jungle far from the turtle and steal their monsters; you can even gank the enemy gold laner.

### Farming routes by phase and jungler type
- [04:17] [T] Before minute 5 the farming route is the same for all junglers (timeline slide "Farming routes the same" 0-5, 04:18-04:25). After minute 5 tank and damage junglers differ (slide "Mid Game Macro").
- Damage jungler [04:27]:
  - [04:27] [T] Clear speed is fast, so focus on clearing jungle monsters and then farming minions alone in an empty lane.
  - [04:37] [T] Usually the minions you take are the XP lane minions, while the XP laner rotates to another lane. Avoid the gold lane (they need to farm too). (Minimap "Damage Jungler Rotation" 04:48 shows the jungler in the lane area beside the XP-lane side.)
  - [04:47] [T] Farm ALONE so your XP does not get shared.
  - [04:52] [T] Purpose of all this: win the retribution fight; the higher your level, the more retribution damage and the easier the retribution fight (see retribution-level examples in the LIVE table).
  - [05:38] [T] Damage junglers get ahead by OUT-FARMING the opponent (slide "Out-farming the opponent").
- Tank jungler [05:08]:
  - [05:09] [T] Clears slowly. Do not farm lane minions (the reason is an item passive that only triggers on jungle monsters; see LIVE table).
  - [05:24] [T] So do not farm lane minions.
  - [05:27] [T] Tank junglers shine in the mid game: enter the enemy jungle to steal their monsters, pressure the enemy and force small fights ("Tank Jungler Route" minimaps 05:30 and 05:25: jungler sits in the enemy-side jungle near the enemy buffs, turtle area visible below).
  - [05:38] [T] Tank junglers get ahead by DISRUPTING the enemy's farm.

### Retribution fight method
- [05:43] [T] Even with a big retribution level advantage you still need to use it well. Three steps (on-screen "How To Retribution Fight", 05:58-06:05):
  1. [05:52] [T] Calculate your last-hit damage = retribution + your hardest-hitting skill.
  2. [06:00] [T] Position yourself properly: do not stand inside the pit or anywhere the enemy can easily target you (06:05 clip: jungler stands on the outer ring/edge of the pit rather than inside).
  3. [06:07] [T] CC the enemy jungler as much as possible right before retribution, so the enemy loses their window.
  - [06:16] [T] If you have no CC, it is fine to ask your team for help.

### Teamfights
- [06:21] [T] The team must be coordinated, especially in teamfights; your role in teamfights depends on your hero.
- Tank jungler:
  - [06:30] [T] Stand at the front line.
  - [06:34] [T] MISTAKE 2/3 (super common): showing yourself on the map and getting picked off (on-screen "Mistake 2/3", 06:35). While you wait to respawn, the enemy team will definitely take the objective.
  - [06:45] [T] So make sure your team is ready to back you up before you show yourself on the map (minimap 06:40 shows the dead/respawning tank at base far from the fight while enemies sit at the Lord pit; 06:55 shows the tank joining allies in the middle).
  - [06:50] [T] Main job: protect your marksman and mage, while preparing CC to punish the enemy marksman if they step out of position.
- Damage jungler (different: go in last):
  - [07:00] [T] Never show yourself on the map. Always hide alone in the bush because a single CC can get you picked off.
  - [07:07] [T] Your job is to wait: wait for the enemy to engage and then flank them, or wait for your tank to start the fight and follow up.
  - [07:23] [T] Target priority: marksman, then jungler, then mage.
  - (Minimap "Damage Jungler Teamfight" 07:20/07:25: the damage jungler waits just outside the clash, then enters the pile at the Lord pit.)

### Late game
- [07:31] [T] Role changes in the late game for a tank jungler: no longer the main tank but the secondary tank. "You are a walking retribution." Show yourself on the map with your team and get ready to fight (on-screen "Late Game Macro: 1. A Tank Jungler is no longer the main Tank, but the secondary", 07:38).
- [07:44] [T] MISTAKE 3/3 (tank jungler): clearing a lane that is far from the Lord. The moment you are seen far from the Lord, the enemy can take it immediately (on-screen "Mistake 3/3" 07:46; minimap 07:52: lord icon top-left, jungler far at the right edge with a dotted line = the long distance).
- [07:54] [T] Damage jungler: you may still clear a far lane if you have high mobility.
  - [08:03] [T] But do not show yourself on the map, because whoever wins CC wins and your team will straight up lose.
  - [08:10] [T] Position yourself as far as possible from the enemy initiator and get ready to fight (minimaps 08:00-08:18: the damage jungler comes from the far lane to the back of own team at the Lord fight; at 08:18 dotted lines from the jungler show the enemies it is staying away from / can reach).
- [08:15] [T] The key to winning the late game is the Lord. You need to understand the "lord dance".

### Lord dance
- [08:22] [T] If the situation is even, the Lord cannot be burst because retribution becomes a 50-50 (slide, 5v5 portraits with the Lord and "50/50", 08:30).
- [08:29] [T] That is why pros use the lord dance so often: executed properly it gives you a retribution advantage (slide "70/30", 08:36).
- Steps ("How To Lord Dance", 08:42-09:20):
  1. [08:37] [T] Slow push the far lane first.
  2. [08:41] [T] Then set up vision.
  3. [08:42] [T] Open the map near the jungle and hit the Lord slowly. Do not stand inside the Lord pit.
  4. [08:48] [T] Hit the Lord while opening the map in the middle of the river until you reveal some enemies.
- [08:53] [T] Goal is NOT to finish the Lord but to make the enemy confused between the Lord and the far lane that is being pushed.
- [09:02] [T] Poke the enemy lightly while you keep stalling the Lord.
- [09:05] [T] The pushed far lane forces the enemy to send someone to catch the wave; this opens a brief window for your team to start a fight.
- [09:15] [T] If you cannot, keep poking lightly and repeat until someone blunders. (Minimaps 08:55-09:20: enemy heroes gather near the Lord pit at the top-left; at 09:02 the map shows the pushed lane near the enemy base on the far right side; own team stays on the river side of the pit and the enemy flickers in/out.)

## Terms & definitions
- Last-hit damage value [03:01] [T]: retribution damage + your hardest-hitting skill (the burst you can apply to finish an objective monster). Compare with the enemy jungler's; the higher one wins the retribution fight.
- Retribution fight [03:18] [T]: contest over who last-hits the objective (turtle/Lord) with retribution.
- Tank jungler [00:25]: Jungler of the tank type; picked when team lacks two tanks; slow clear, disrupts enemy farm, front-line in fights, secondary tank late. Damage jungler: assassin or fighter jungler; fast clear, out-farms, waits to enter last.
- Dive comp / front-to-back comp [00:50]: team comp types that guide jungler pick (jumper vs DPS jungler).
- Early Game Rotation [02:40]: after clearing jungle monsters, moving to the XP lane to join the XP laner.
- "Crush the map" [04:06]: weaker-matchup jungler goes into enemy jungle far from the turtle, steals monsters and may gank the enemy gold laner.
- Lord dance [08:15]: stalling the Lord at low pressure while pushing the far lane and poking, to split the enemy's attention and gain a retribution advantage.
- "Walking retribution" [07:41]: the late-game tank jungler's role as a secondary tank who shows with the team.
- Mistake 1/3, 2/3, 3/3: clearing minions in the early game / tank jungler showing on the map and getting picked off / clearing a lane far from the Lord in the late game.

## Worked examples
- Leomord vs Baxia, turtle (Round 1/2, [03:18]): Leomord's last-hit damage is higher. Leomord: focus the turtle while roamer + XP laner zone. Baxia: cannot contest the turtle; teamfight first, Lord later.
- Ling vs Baxia, no last-hit and no fight edge (Round 2/2, [03:54]): Ling goes to the enemy jungle far from the turtle, steals monsters, can gank the gold laner.
- Invade (matchup rule, [02:17]): invaded and you win the matchup -> fight; lose -> do not die, retreat and farm elsewhere; same logic when you invade.
- Retribution level advantage still needs execution ([05:43]): compute last-hit damage, stand outside the pit, CC the enemy jungler right before retribution.
- Tank jungler teamfight mistake ([06:34]): showing early and getting picked off -> respawn timer -> enemy takes objective. Fix: show only when team can back you up.
- Late-game damage jungler at far lane ([07:54]-[08:10]): fine if mobile, but stay off the map and finish positioned as far as possible from the enemy initiator.
- Lord dance ([08:37]-[09:20]): slow push far lane -> vision -> hit the Lord slowly from outside the pit while map is open in the river middle -> poke -> enemy must send someone to catch the wave -> team fights in that window; otherwise repeat.
